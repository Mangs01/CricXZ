Vaibhav Sooryavanshi: The Rise of a 15-Year-Old Phenomenon

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's meteoric rise through the Indian cricket system has been compressed into an unusually short timeline. Making his First-Class debut for Bihar at the age of just 12 years and 284 days, he quickly demonstrated that age is merely a number when matched with generational talent.

At senior international level, Vaibhav has scored 295 runs in 10 T20Is at a calculated strike rate of 185.53, including two half-centuries and a highest score of 81. His breakout IPL 2026 season was even more explosive: 776 runs in 16 matches at a strike rate of 237.30, with 72 sixes and the Orange Cap.

He broke the internet in April 2025 when he became the youngest centurion in IPL history (101 off 38 balls), which also stood as the fastest IPL century by an Indian batter (35 balls). Recently, he was a key contributor in the Asian Games 2026, bringing his fearless striking to the global stage.

Current Status: Bihar Captaincy Milestone

On 14 November 2026 at Holkar Stadium in Indore, Sooryavanshi led Bihar against Saurashtra in the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy. At 15 years and 232 days, he passed Croatia's Zach Vukusic (17 years and 311 days) as the youngest captain in men's T20 cricket. Read the captaincy record and match report.

Captaincy debut: 18 off 12

He struck two fours and one six before Chetan Sakariya caught him off his own bowling. Bihar made 137/9; Saurashtra reached 138/5 in 16.3 overs to win by five wickets.

Latest T20I record

His senior India record stands at 295 runs from 10 T20Is, averaging 29.50, with a highest score of 81 and two fifties. His latest T20I innings was 45 from 19 balls against New Zealand on 27 October 2026.

Availability after the opener

Reports differ on whether he will also lead against Railways on 16 November. Rajasthan Royals lists the second fixture, while Cricbuzz quotes Bihar chief selector Harvinder Singh saying he is available only for the opener before India A duty. A second captaincy appearance is not treated as confirmed here.
