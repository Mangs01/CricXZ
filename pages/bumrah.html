Jasprit Bumrah Test Career, Wickets & Record

As of 27 August 2026, Jasprit Bumrah has played 52 Test matches for India and taken 234 wickets in 99 bowling innings. He has a Test bowling average of 19.79, an economy rate of 2.77 and best innings figures of 6/27.

Bumrah has taken 16 five-wicket hauls in Test innings. His best match figures are 9/86, reflecting his record as one of India's leading fast-bowling options in Test cricket.

He became the fastest Indian fast bowler to reach 150 Test wickets, achieving the milestone in his 34th Test. Bumrah also became the first Indian fast bowler to reach No. 1 in the ICC Men's Test Bowling Rankings.

Career Overview

Bumrah made his ODI and T20I debuts against Australia in January 2016 before making his Test debut against South Africa in January 2018. His verified career includes international honours in Test and T20I cricket and appearances for India across all three formats.

Test Career

Bumrah made his Test debut at Newlands on 5 January 2018. He became the fastest Indian fast bowler to reach 150 Test wickets, completing the milestone in his 34th Test, and became the first Indian fast bowler to reach No. 1 in the ICC Men’s Test Bowling Rankings.

ODI Career

Bumrah made his ODI debut against Australia at Sydney Cricket Ground on 23 January 2016. His verified ODI career statistics are listed above using the BCCI baseline dated 27 August 2026.

T20I Career

Bumrah made his T20I debut against Australia at Adelaide Oval on 26 January 2016. He was Player of the Tournament when India won the 2024 ICC Men’s T20 World Cup and Player of the Match in the 2026 final against New Zealand after taking 4/15.
