⚡ The Asian Games 2026 Final Heroics

While chasing a massive target of 216 against India, Hasan Nawaz played the innings of his life, smashing 96 runs off just 51 deliveries. Tape-Ball Sensation to International Star

Raised in an environment with minimal cricket infrastructure, Hasan Nawaz initially honed his skills playing tape-ball cricket before moving to Islamabad to pursue formal training. At just 14, he joined the Lashings Cricket Club and steadily progressed through the Islamabad Cricket Association (ICA) system.

After making his PSL debut for Islamabad United in 2023, Hasan made his T20I debut for Pakistan in March 2025. His Quetta Gladiators season followed: the PCB records 399 runs in 12 matches and 10 innings in PSL 2025, including an unbeaten 100 against Islamabad United in May. That PSL hundred came after his maiden international century, rather than causing his earlier March selection.

In his third T20I, Hasan scored an unbeaten 105 off 45 balls against New Zealand at Eden Park, Auckland, on 21 March 2025. He reached his hundred from 44 balls, setting Pakistan’s fastest men’s T20I century record at the time. His separate franchise experience includes Antigua and Barbuda Falcons.
