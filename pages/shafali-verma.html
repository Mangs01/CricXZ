Shafali Verma International Career & Batting Records

As of 13 September 2026, Shafali Verma has scored 4,522 runs in international cricket across Tests, ODIs and T20Is. Her career totals stand at 640 Test runs, 745 ODI runs and 3,137 T20I runs.

Her standout Test achievement is a score of 205 against South Africa in Chennai in 2024. The double century came from 194 balls, making it the fastest double hundred in women’s Test cricket at the time.

During the Women’s Asia Cup 2026, Verma also became the youngest player to reach 3,000 women’s T20I runs. She crossed the milestone against Pakistan at the age of 22 years and 220 days.

Her attacking approach at the top of the order has made her an important part of India’s batting plans across formats.

Verma finished the Women’s Asia Cup 2026 with a tournament-leading 236 runs from five innings and three half-centuries. Her 68 from 39 balls in the final helped India beat Sri Lanka by 72 runs and win an eighth title.
