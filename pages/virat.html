Virat Kohli International Career Stats and Records

As of 4 November 2026, Kohli has played 566 international matches and scored 28,601 runs: 9,230 in Tests, 15,183 in ODIs and 4,188 in T20Is. His 86 international centuries comprise 30 in Tests, 55 in ODIs and one in T20Is.

His highest scores remain 254 not out in Tests, 183 in ODIs and 122 not out in T20Is. Test and T20I totals are final; ODI totals include the Auckland match on 4 November.

ODI sixes are under verification because the retrieved career sources disagree. An aggregate international sixes total is withheld until that discrepancy is resolved.

Historical India Leadership

TEST

India captaincy records

Kohli captained India in 68 Tests and recorded 40 wins; both are India records.

2018–19

Test-series victory in Australia

He led India to its first Test-series victory in Australia.
