Shreyas Iyer's International Career

Shreyas Iyer made his international debut in 2017 and established himself as a specialist middle-order batter across formats. His ODI record is the strongest statistical part of his international career, with more than 3,100 runs, five centuries and 24 half-centuries.

In 2026, Iyer became India's T20I captain. He led India to the Asian Games men's cricket gold medal before beginning the home series against West Indies with a match-winning century in Lucknow.

Maiden T20I Century: 102* Against West Indies

Iyer struck an unbeaten 102 from 43 balls in Lucknow on 6 October 2026. He reached his maiden T20I hundred from 42 deliveries, hit 10 fours and six sixes, and shared an unbroken 137-run partnership with Ishan Kishan.

The innings powered India to 172/2 in 14.4 overs and an eight-wicket victory. Iyer was named Player of the Match. Read the complete India vs West Indies first T20I match report.
