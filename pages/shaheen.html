Shaheen Afridi: Pakistan's Fast-Bowling Spearhead

Shaheen Afridi remains one of the most feared new-ball bowlers in world cricket. His ability to bring the ball back into right-handers at extreme pace has resulted in numerous devastating first-over spells across international cricket.

Pakistan selected a separate squad for the 2026 Asian Games, led by Sahibzada Farhan. Afridi was not included in the PCB's updated 15-member squad announced on 20 September. This profile therefore does not credit him with a performance in the final.

In 2021, he achieved global superstardom by becoming the youngest bowler to win the prestigious Sir Garfield Sobers Trophy for the ICC Men's Cricketer of the Year.

Statistics Scope and Correction

Correction, 16 November 2026: An earlier version incorrectly credited Afridi with 3/35 in the Asian Games final and described static figures as live updates. The PCB squad announcement and Olympic Council of Asia report do not support that attribution. The T20I aggregate was temporarily withdrawn; it has now been replaced with the reconciled 103-match, 136-wicket record. Test and ODI totals have also been refreshed from the cited career tables.

How the rates reconcile: Test: 3,545 runs conceded from 6,684 legal balls for 126 wickets. ODI: 3,794 runs from 4,109 balls for 156 wickets. T20I: 2,904 runs from 2,223 balls for 136 wickets. T20I economy is 2,904 × 6 ÷ 2,223 = 7.838..., shown here as 7.84; Cricket Australia displays 7.83. The underlying overs and runs agree. In cricket notation, 370.3 overs means 370 complete overs plus three balls, not 370.3 decimal overs.

Reading the table: Bowling innings count occasions on which he bowled, rather than matches played. BBI is best bowling in one innings; BBM is best bowling across an entire Test. A five-wicket innings and a ten-wicket match are different achievements.
