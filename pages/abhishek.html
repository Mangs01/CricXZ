Abhishek Sharma's 113 Powers India to Series Win

After India lost Sanju Samson early, Abhishek attacked immediately and reached fifty from only 18 deliveries. He finished with 113 from 30 balls, while adding 108 with Ishan Kishan from just 40 balls.

The innings carried him beyond 2,000 T20I runs and helped India post 223. Arshdeep Singh's 3/44 then completed a 14-run win. Read the full India vs New Zealand third T20I report.

Abhishek Sharma: The Fearless Powerplay Specialist

Abhishek Sharma rose to global prominence following a historic IPL 2024 season with the Sunrisers Hyderabad, where he dismantled bowling attacks with ease. Earning his national call-up soon after, he proved his mettle on the international stage by smashing a 46-ball century in just his second T20I match against Zimbabwe.

As a left-handed opener, his approach is built on pure hand-eye coordination and immense power against both pace and spin. After his breathtaking 20-ball fifty in the Asian Games 2026 Final, he continued to build his reputation as an attacking powerplay batter.

Abhishek Sharma T20I Sixes and Power-Hitting Records

After the New Zealand third T20I on 27 October 2026, Abhishek Sharma had hit 158 sixes in 70 T20Is. Career totals change as he plays further matches.

In Wellington he scored 113 from 38 balls, striking 11 sixes and nine fours. He reached his century off 35 balls and shared a 133-run opening partnership with Vaibhav Sooryavanshi.

Against Afghanistan in Delhi on 17 September 2026, he scored 108 from 34 balls with 11 sixes. The BCCI reported his 30-ball century as the fastest by an Indian in men's T20Is.
