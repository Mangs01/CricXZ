Abhishek Sharma: The Fearless Powerplay Specialist

Abhishek Sharma rose to global prominence following a historic IPL 2024 season with the Sunrisers Hyderabad, where he dismantled bowling attacks with ease. Earning his national call-up soon after, he proved his mettle on the international stage by smashing a 46-ball century in just his second T20I match against Zimbabwe.

As a left-handed opener, his approach is built on pure hand-eye coordination and immense power against both pace and spin. In T20Is, his strike rate hovering near 180 makes him one of the most dangerous batters in the powerplay. Alongside players like Shubman Gill and Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Abhishek represents the new, fearless era of Indian batting.

Besides his explosive batting, Abhishek is a handy left-arm orthodox spinner, frequently breaking partnerships in the middle overs for Punjab in domestic cricket and providing crucial balance to the Team India lineup.
