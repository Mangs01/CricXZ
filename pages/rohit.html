Rohit Sharma ODI Career

Rohit Sharma made his ODI debut for India against Ireland on 23 June 2007. He later established himself as an opening batter and developed into one of India's most successful ODI run-scorers.

As of 03 October 2026, Rohit has played 291 ODIs and scored 12,120 runs in 283 innings at an average of 49.27 and a strike rate of 93.38. His ODI record includes 35 centuries and 63 half-centuries, with a highest score of 264.

Rohit's 264 against Sri Lanka in 2014 remains the highest individual score in men's ODI cricket. He is also the only batter to have scored three ODI double-centuries.

Rohit has also had a major leadership role in India's ODI history. He captained India to the 2023 ICC Men's Cricket World Cup final and the 2025 ICC Champions Trophy title. As of 03 October 2026, he remains active in ODI cricket as a batter and does not hold India's current international captaincy.

How to Read Rohit Sharma’s Career Records

International totals combine Tests, ODIs and T20Is. They exclude IPL, other franchise cricket and domestic matches. At the displayed cutoff, 4,301 Test runs, 12,120 ODI runs and 4,231 T20I runs add up to 20,652 international runs.

The same distinction matters for sixes: 88 in Tests, 381 in ODIs and 205 in T20Is add up to 674. ODI sixes and international sixes therefore answer different questions. Batting average uses dismissals, while strike rate measures runs per 100 balls; neither is simply runs divided by matches.
