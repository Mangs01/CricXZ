Babar Azam: A Modern Batting Maestro

At the 2 October 2026 cutoff, Babar Azam had amassed 15,993 international runs. He boasts an exceptional ODI average of 53.44 with 6,626 runs and 20 centuries. In Test cricket, he has aggregated 4,771 runs at an average of 42.98, including 9 hundreds and a career-best of 196 against Australia in 2022.

His T20I record is equally impressive. In 136 batting innings, he scored 4,596 runs at an average of 38.95. Babar's batting role for Pakistan should not be confused with participation in every national-team event. He was not named in the PCB's updated 15-member squad for the 2026 Asian Games, which was captained by Sahibzada Farhan.

Babar previously held the No. 1 ranking for ODI batters, surpassing Virat Kohli's long-standing reign in 2021. His 32 international centuries cement his legacy as one of Pakistan's all-time greats.

Reading the Historical Statistics

Cutoff: 2 October 2026. Reconciled: 16 November 2026. Later appearances are excluded. The November Test added 84 runs from 106 balls in two dismissed innings; removing it from the 67-Test aggregate gives this snapshot of 66 Tests, 122 innings, 4,771 runs and 8,613 balls. The five ODI innings from 18 to 31 October added 118 runs from 159 balls, with no not-outs; removing them gives 143 ODIs, 140 innings, 6,626 runs and 7,652 balls.

Rate calculations: Batting average is runs divided by dismissals, not matches or innings alone. This snapshot has 11 Test not-outs, 16 ODI not-outs and 18 T20I not-outs. The averages are 4,771 ÷ 111 = 42.98, 6,626 ÷ 124 = 53.44 and 4,596 ÷ 118 = 38.95, rounded to two decimals. Strike rate is runs per 100 balls: Test 55.39, ODI 86.59 and T20I 128.02 using 3,590 T20I balls.

Source differences: Cricket Australia displays a T20I strike rate of 128.06; Cricbuzz supplies 3,590 balls, which yields 128.02. We use the explicit ball count for this calculation. The T20I match-count difference (145 versus 144) remains disclosed above rather than silently resolved.

Career totals at this cutoff: 4,771 + 6,626 + 4,596 = 15,993 international runs; 9 + 20 + 3 = 32 centuries. PCB reported his 16,000-run milestone during the 18 October ODI, after this snapshot.
