Current Status

As of 27 August 2026, Hardik Pandya is an India international all-rounder whose recent international career has been concentrated in ODI and T20I cricket. He was part of India’s title-winning squads at the 2024 and 2026 ICC Men’s T20 World Cups and the 2025 ICC Champions Trophy. His most recent Test appearance remains from 2018.
