How to Read the CricXZ Score Center

The score center groups matches by their current status. This helps readers separate in-progress score information from scheduled fixtures and completed results.

Live matches

A live label means play has started. Runs, wickets and overs may update after a short provider or network delay.

Upcoming fixtures

Upcoming cards identify scheduled matches that have not started. Final playing XIs remain subject to the toss.

Latest results

Result cards reproduce the provider's match status. CricXZ reports separately verify important results and performances against cited coverage.

CricXZ does not treat an automated score card as a complete match report. Major results, Player of the Match awards and notable records are checked against cited match centres or reputable reports before publication in the News section.
