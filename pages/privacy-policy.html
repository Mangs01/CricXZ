Privacy Policy

How CricXZ handles information, cookies and advertising technologies.

Effective date: 23 September 2026

Overview

CricXZ operates the website cricxz.com. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use our cricket news, live-score and player-profile services, why it may be used, and the choices available to you.

Information You Provide

If you contact us by email, we may receive your name, email address and the information included in your message. We use this information only to respond, manage feedback, investigate corrections or handle legitimate enquiries.

Please do not send sensitive personal, financial or identification information through our contact channels.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, CricXZ and its service providers may automatically receive limited technical information such as browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, approximate location based on IP address, pages visited and interaction timestamps. This information may be used for security, diagnostics, audience measurement and improving site performance.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

CricXZ may use cookies, local storage, web beacons or similar technologies to remember preferences, understand site usage, protect the service and support advertising. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect certain site features.

Google Advertising

We may use Google AdSense to display advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to CricXZ or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on those visits.

Visitors may manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. More information about how Google uses data on partner sites is available at Google's partner-sites policy.

Depending on location and applicable law, we may display a consent message before using certain advertising or measurement technologies.

Third-Party Services and Links

Our pages may link to cricket boards, governing bodies, news providers, statistics services, social platforms and other external websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by their own policies. CricXZ is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party sites.

Data Retention and Security

We retain correspondence and related information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, legal compliance, security or dispute resolution. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

CricXZ is a general-audience cricket website and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us to request review and deletion.

Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask about personal information you have provided to us or request its correction or deletion. You may also adjust browser cookie settings and advertising preferences. We may need reasonable information to verify and process a request.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when our services, legal obligations or third-party tools change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest published version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@cricxz.com.
