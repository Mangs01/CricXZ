Kuldeep Yadav: The Magician of Spin

At the 2 October 2026 snapshot used above, Kuldeep had 200 ODI wickets in 123 matches, averaging 26.76 with an economy rate of 5.08. His T20I record at the same cutoff was 95 wickets in 54 matches, averaging 13.75 with an economy rate of 6.95.

His capacity to extract sharp turn and drift makes him highly effective in middle overs, regularly breaking crucial partnerships. His recent standout performances include a stellar showing at the 2025 Asia Cup, where he claimed 17 wickets in 7 matches at an average of 9.29, finishing as the leading wicket-taker. Abhishek Sharma received the Player of the Tournament award. He was also a member of the World Cup-winning Indian squad in 2024.

The Test snapshot records 80 wickets in 19 matches, including five five-wicket innings. These are format-specific career figures at the displayed cutoff, rather than a live career counter.

How to Read This Statistics Snapshot

Cutoff: 2 October 2026. Reconciled: 16 November 2026. The review date does not change the statistical cutoff. Later ODI and T20I appearances are excluded so that the table and narrative describe the same point in his career.

Matches and bowling innings are different counts: a player can appear without bowling. Bowling average measures runs conceded per wicket; economy measures runs conceded per six-ball over. Five-wicket innings count occasions on which a bowler took at least five wickets in one innings, rather than wickets across a whole Test match.
