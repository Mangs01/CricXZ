Editorial Policy

Our standards for accurate, transparent and useful cricket coverage.

Effective date: 23 September 2026

Editorial Mission

CricXZ aims to provide clear, timely and useful cricket coverage for readers worldwide. Our content includes match reports and previews, player profiles, career statistics, records, rankings and explanatory updates.

Research and Source Standards

We prioritize official and primary sources where available, including the International Cricket Council, national cricket boards, tournament organizers, official scorecards and verified team or player communications. Reliable secondary reporting may be used for context or when primary information is unavailable.

Articles containing time-sensitive facts should identify their publication or update date. Source links are included where they materially help readers verify important claims.

Statistics and Historical Records

Player statistics and records are checked against reputable cricket databases or official sources before publication. Because databases can apply different record definitions or update schedules, CricXZ may identify the statistical cutoff date and relevant format. We update material inaccuracies when reliable new information becomes available.

Originality and Attribution

CricXZ publishes original summaries and editorial explanations. We do not present another publisher's reporting or creative work as our own. Facts gathered from external sources are independently written and, where appropriate, attributed through descriptive source links.

Editorial Images

Images identified as “CricXZ editorial illustrations” are original illustrative graphics created for presentation and storytelling. They may be digitally composed or AI-assisted and should not be interpreted as documentary photographs of the exact moment shown. Factual score, date and record information displayed within graphics is checked separately from the illustrative scene.

Corrections and Updates

We correct confirmed errors promptly and make substantive factual updates when a story materially changes. Minor spelling, formatting or clarity edits may be made without a separate correction note. When an update significantly changes the article's factual substance, an updated date may be displayed.

Readers can report a possible error by emailing info@cricxz.com with the page URL, disputed information and a supporting source. We review correction requests against reliable evidence.

Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions are based on relevance, accuracy and reader value. Advertising or commercial relationships do not grant control over our editorial conclusions. Sponsored or paid content, if introduced, will be clearly identified.

Conflicts and Impartiality

We aim to distinguish verified facts from analysis and avoid misleading or sensational presentation. CricXZ is an independent publication and is not officially affiliated with the ICC, BCCI, any national cricket board, team, league or player unless expressly stated.

Contact the Editorial Desk

For corrections, sourcing questions or editorial feedback, contact info@cricxz.com.
