The Complete Multi-Format Player

Rahul made his Test debut in December 2014 and his ODI debut in June 2016, scoring an unbeaten hundred in that first ODI. He went on to make centuries in all three international formats. His ability to open or bat in the middle order is a useful distinction when reading records from different stages of his career.

At the displayed cutoff, the Test table records 4,288 runs with a highest score of 199. His ODI record stands at 3,541 runs in 101 appearances, averaging 51.32. Average uses dismissals rather than appearances: an unbeaten innings adds runs without adding a dismissal.

The IPL is a separate franchise competition, so its 5,815 career runs are not included in an international total. His IPL high score of 152* came for Delhi Capitals against Punjab Kings in April 2026; that verified innings remains distinct from his earlier 132* for Punjab in 2020.
