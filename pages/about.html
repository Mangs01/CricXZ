About CricXZ

Independent cricket news, verified match coverage and player records.

Who We Are

CricXZ is an independent cricket publishing website based in India. We cover international cricket, Team India, women's cricket, major tournaments and player careers through news reports, explainers and statistics-led profiles. Our aim is to make important cricket developments easy to understand without sacrificing factual accuracy or source transparency.

Our Mission

Our mission is to publish useful, accessible and responsibly sourced cricket information. A CricXZ article should do more than repeat a score: it should explain the match context, identify the decisive performances and help readers understand what the result or announcement means next.

Our Vision

We are building a focused cricket reference and news publication with clear navigation, mobile-friendly pages and connected coverage. Player profiles, match reports and related stories are internally linked so readers can follow a career, series or tournament beyond a single headline.

How We Work

CricXZ Sports Desk checks match scores, squads, schedules, appointments and records against cricket boards, governing bodies, official team or tournament channels and established sports-news organisations. Sources are listed on detailed news reports whenever they materially support the story.

We distinguish confirmed information from previews and predictions. When a match finishes or an official update changes an earlier report, we update the relevant page or publish a clearly dated follow-up. Generated editorial graphics are used to illustrate coverage and are identified as CricXZ editorial artwork rather than match photography.

Editorial Standards and Corrections

Accuracy, attribution and readable context guide our editorial decisions. Our Editorial Policy explains how stories are researched, reviewed, updated and corrected. If a reader identifies a possible factual error, we ask them to send the page URL and a reliable supporting source to info@cricxz.com.

Corrections that materially affect a story are reviewed promptly. We do not accept payment in exchange for changing match facts, player records or editorial conclusions.

Ownership and Contact

CricXZ is independently operated from India. Editorial coverage is published under the CricXZ Sports Desk byline unless an individual contributor is identified. Readers, rights holders and organisations can reach us through the Contact page.

Our Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions and Disclaimer explain how the website operates and how its information should be used.
