India vs West Indies 2nd ODI: India Look to Seal Series in Guwahati

India enter the second ODI with a 1-0 series lead after Virat Kohli and Shubman Gill scored centuries in the opener. West Indies now need a response to keep the three-match series alive.

Published 30 September 2026By CricXZ Sports DeskMatch Preview

India face West Indies in the second ODI of their three-match series at Barsapara Cricket Stadium in Guwahati on September 30. India lead the series 1-0 after winning the opening match in Thiruvananthapuram by eight wickets.

The match gives India an opportunity to take an unassailable 2-0 lead, while West Indies need a victory to take the series into a decider in New Chandigarh.

India vs West Indies 2nd ODI: Match Details

Date: 30 September 2026

Time: 2:00 PM IST

Venue: Barsapara Cricket Stadium, Guwahati

Series: India lead 1-0

India Enter Guwahati With a 1-0 Lead

India produced a strong all-round performance in the first ODI. West Indies posted 295/7, but India chased 296 in 41.4 overs with eight wickets in hand.

Virat Kohli scored an unbeaten 139 from 88 balls and reached his 55th ODI century while crossing 15,000 ODI runs. Shubman Gill also made 110 from 108 balls, while Kuldeep Yadav took 4/40.

Kohli and Gill Remain in Focus

Kohli's first-ODI hundred of the series came during a controlled chase, while Gill continued his run of important contributions as India's ODI captain. Their partnership in the opener helped India turn a target of 296 into a comfortable win.

West Indies will need a plan for India's top order after the visitors struggled to defend a competitive first-innings total.

West Indies Look for a Stronger Response

Justin Greaves scored 101 in the opening ODI and John Campbell added 62, helping West Indies reach 295/7. Amir Jangoo also contributed an unbeaten 58 late in the innings.

The visitors will need greater support around their leading batters and will also look to make better use of opportunities in the field after India completed the chase with significant room to spare.

Guwahati Venue Adds Another Dimension

Barsapara Cricket Stadium is hosting the second ODI of the series. The venue has featured high-scoring limited-overs cricket, making the toss, powerplay batting and middle-over bowling important areas to watch.

India's Squad for the ODI Series

BCCI's announced India ODI squad includes Shubman Gill (captain), Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli, Ruturaj Gaikwad, KL Rahul, Dhruv Jurel, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Ravindra Jadeja, Kuldeep Yadav, Prasidh Krishna, Gurnoor Brar, Mohammed Siraj, Auqib Nabi, Yashasvi Jaiswal and Naman Dhir.

What to Watch in the Second ODI

  • India's top order: Kohli, Gill and Rohit Sharma will be central to the batting contest.
  • Kuldeep Yadav: His middle-overs spell was a key difference in the first ODI.
  • West Indies' response: The visitors need a stronger bowling and fielding performance.
  • Series pressure: A win for India would make the series 2-0, while a West Indies win would force a decider.
