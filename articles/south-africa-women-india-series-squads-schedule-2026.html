South Africa Women Name India Series Squads; Wolvaardt to Lead Across Formats

Laura Wolvaardt leads the hosts across formats as new names earn opportunities for December’s India series.

24 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskWomen’s Squad Update

AI-generated CricXZ editorial illustration; not an actual match photograph. Artwork illustrates the squad update.

South Africa Women have announced three squads for India’s December 2026 visit, with Laura Wolvaardt captaining the hosts in Tests, ODIs and T20Is. The 24 November announcement covers a one-off Test followed by three matches in each white-ball format.

Series at a Glance

Tour window: 9–30 December 2026
Formats: One Test, three ODIs and three T20Is
South Africa captain: Laura Wolvaardt across all three formats
Opening fixture: Test at St George’s Park, Gqeberha.

Where the New Call-Ups Fit

Alexandra Candler has earned her first national selection in the Test and ODI groups. Oluhle Siyo is included for the Test, while former Namibia international Yasmeen Khan joins the T20I squad.

Caitlin Wyngaard, already capped in T20Is, is selected for the Test and ODI formats. Marizanne Kapp and Sinalo Jafta are also included across the three squads.

A Return to Red-Ball Cricket

The ICC notes that this will be South Africa Women’s first Test since 2024. The four-day fixture opens the tour before the teams move to the ODI and T20I legs.

For India’s recent leadership background, read our earlier captaincy report. It is separate from this announcement of South Africa’s squads.

CricXZ Analysis: One Tour, Three Selection Tests

A multi-format visit asks teams to adapt more than their scoring tempo. The Test requires sustained spells and long partnerships; the white-ball matches change the balance between preserving wickets, controlling the middle overs and finding boundaries.

The new call-ups make selection worth following, but a squad place alone cannot tell readers who will debut or how many matches a player will receive. Confirmed XIs and subsequent availability updates will provide that evidence. This section is CricXZ editorial analysis.
