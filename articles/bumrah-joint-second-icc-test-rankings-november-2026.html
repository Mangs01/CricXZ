Bumrah Rises to Joint No.2 in ICC Test Rankings; Pant, Gill and Jaiswal Gain

Bumrah leads India’s bowling gains as three Indian batters climb inside the top ten.

Jasprit Bumrah has climbed three places to joint second in the ICC Test bowling rankings. The ICC’s 25 November 2026 release also records gains for Rishabh Pant, Shubman Gill and Yashasvi Jaiswal among Test batters.

Bumrah Joins Robinson in Second Place

Bumrah’s five wickets in the drawn Wellington Test helped him rise into a tie with England’s Ollie Robinson. Mitchell Starc remains the leading bowler in this update.

Pant, Gill and Jaiswal Move Up

Pant moves up one position to sixth among batters, Gill climbs three to seventh and Jaiswal gains five to ninth. These batting positions belong to a separate list from Bumrah’s bowling rank.

How to Read This Update

A position and a movement answer different questions. The position tells readers where a player sits in the published list; the movement describes the change from the preceding list. A rise of three places does not mean a player earned three rating points.

The word “joint” also matters. It identifies a shared position rather than an outright second place. Comparing the four names in the table requires attention to the category column: a batter’s sixth place cannot be compared directly with a bowler’s second place as though both belonged to one combined ladder.

CricXZ Analysis: Recognition and the Next Contest

For readers following India, the useful feature of this snapshot is the spread of movement across bowling and batting. It offers several individual storylines to follow, while the next match still has to be judged through what happens on the field. This is CricXZ editorial interpretation.

A rankings headline can provide context before a Test, but it cannot tell a captain which combination to choose. Selection involves roles and conditions as well as individual standing. Nor does a ranking movement turn a drawn match into a victory: individual recognition and a team result describe different things.
