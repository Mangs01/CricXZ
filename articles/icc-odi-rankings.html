ICC ODI Rankings 2026: India leads annual team rankings update

India retained the No.1 position in the ICC Men's ODI Team Rankings annual update published on 11 May 2026, ahead of New Zealand and Australia.

📅 11 May 2026 ✍️ CricXZ Sports Desk 🌍 ICC ODI Cricket

India retained the top position in the ICC Men's ODI Team Rankings after the annual rankings update published by the International Cricket Council on 11 May 2026. India were rated at 118 points, ahead of New Zealand on 113 and Australia on 109.

India remains No.1 in the 2026 annual ODI update

The ICC's May 2026 annual update kept India at the top of the Men's ODI Team Rankings. New Zealand remained second, while Australia held third place.

South Africa moved ahead of Pakistan into fourth place in the same annual update, with South Africa rated at 102 points.

ICC Men's ODI Team Rankings - 11 May 2026

1. India - 118 rating points
2. New Zealand - 113 rating points
3. Australia - 109 rating points
4. South Africa - 102 rating points

What the annual rankings update means

ICC team rankings are based on results accumulated over a multi-year period rather than on a single series or tournament. The annual update adjusts the weighting of older results so that more recent performances have a greater influence on a team's rating.

For the May 2026 update, matches played from May 2025 onward were weighted at 100 percent, while results from the preceding two years were weighted at 50 percent.

Team rankings can change after the annual update

The figures above refer specifically to the official ICC annual update published on 11 May 2026. ODI team ratings can change as further international matches are played, so later live rankings may show different rating points or positions.

This date-specific approach is important when comparing rankings because the ICC table is designed to move with international results throughout the season.

How ICC player rankings work

ICC player rankings for batting, bowling and all-round performance use a points-based rating system. They are not determined only by a player's most recent match. Career performances are taken into account, with greater emphasis placed on more recent performances.

Men's player rankings are normally updated weekly, so individual positions can change as players produce new performances in international cricket.

Why India's No.1 position matters

India's position at the top of the May 2026 annual ODI rankings reflected results accumulated across the ranking period. The margin over New Zealand was five rating points at the time of the update.

Future bilateral series and ICC events can continue to affect the table, which means the rankings should always be read together with the date on which they were published.
