Sourav Ganguly Appointed Delhi Capitals Head Coach for IPL 2027

The former India captain begins a new chapter with Delhi Capitals after shaping the franchise in several leadership roles since 2019.

Published 6 October 2026By CricXZ Sports DeskIPL News

Delhi Capitals have appointed former India captain Sourav Ganguly as their head coach ahead of the 2027 Indian Premier League season. The franchise confirmed the decision on 6 October, bringing a familiar cricket leader into its most influential on-field planning role.

For Ganguly, the job represents his first stint as a head coach in the IPL. For Delhi, it is a move built around continuity: he already understands the owners, management, players and the wider Capitals cricket structure.

Delhi Capitals welcome Ganguly back as head coach

Delhi Capitals said Ganguly will lead the team into the next IPL season with the aim of building a strong and fearless side. In the franchise announcement, he described DC as a team close to his heart and highlighted his long working relationship with its cricket and management groups.

Chairman and co-owner Parth Jindal backed Ganguly's leadership and experience, while co-owner Kiran Kumar Grandhi pointed to his detailed knowledge of the franchise. Those qualities should allow the new coach to begin planning without the usual adjustment period that comes with an external appointment.

A Delhi Capitals association that began in 2019

Ganguly first joined Delhi Capitals as an advisor in 2019. He later held senior responsibilities across the Capitals ecosystem, including Director of Cricket roles connected with the men's and women's teams.

That history makes the appointment more than a high-profile name arriving for a single season. Ganguly has already been involved in recruitment discussions, squad strategy and the development of the franchise's cricket identity. Supporters can also explore the former captain's wider playing legacy in the Sourav Ganguly career profile.

Ganguly replaces Hemang Badani

The appointment ends Hemang Badani's two-season tenure as Delhi Capitals head coach. DC finished sixth in IPL 2026 after taking fifth place in 2025, results that kept them outside the playoffs and increased the demand for a new direction.

Delhi Capitals have yet to win the IPL title. The immediate challenge for Ganguly is therefore clear: turn an experienced and talented squad into a more consistent unit across the league stage, especially in tight matches where small tactical decisions often determine the result.

What Ganguly can bring to DC in IPL 2027

Ganguly built his reputation as a bold captain who trusted young players and encouraged an aggressive style. As head coach, those instincts will need to be paired with detailed T20 planning: defining roles, improving match-ups, managing workloads and finding the right balance between established names and emerging talent.

His familiarity with the franchise can help establish clear communication between the coaching group, leadership core and recruitment team. Delhi's preparations will also connect closely with the wider IPL 2027 retention and auction picture, where every squad decision can shape the season.

Coaching staff details remain to be confirmed

Several reports have linked former players with possible roles in Ganguly's support staff, but Delhi Capitals had not announced a complete coaching group at the time of publication. Any names beyond the confirmed head-coach appointment should therefore be treated as speculation until the franchise makes an official statement.

That distinction matters because the final support structure—batting, bowling, fielding and performance roles—will influence how Ganguly's plans are delivered throughout the tournament.

Key facts

  • Sourav Ganguly has been appointed Delhi Capitals head coach for IPL 2027.
  • He first joined the franchise as an advisor in 2019.
  • He has also served in Director of Cricket roles across the Capitals ecosystem.
  • This will be his first season as an IPL head coach.
  • He replaces Hemang Badani after Delhi finished sixth in IPL 2026.
  • The franchise has not yet confirmed Ganguly's complete support staff.
