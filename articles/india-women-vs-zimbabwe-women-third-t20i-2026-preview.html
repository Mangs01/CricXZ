India Women vs Zimbabwe Women 3rd T20I Preview: Mandhana's Team Eyes 3-0 Sweep

India return to Raipur with an unassailable 2-0 lead after Shafali Verma and Smriti Mandhana powered a dominant nine-wicket victory in the second T20I.

19 October 2026CricXZ Sports DeskMatch Preview

CricXZ editorial illustration for the India Women-Zimbabwe Women third T20I in Raipur.

India Women will target a 3-0 series sweep when they meet Zimbabwe Women in the third and final T20I at the Shaheed Veer Narayan Singh International Stadium in Raipur on 20 October 2026. The match begins at 7:00 PM IST.

Smriti Mandhana's team has already secured the series. The finale now offers India an opportunity to maintain momentum, test combinations and give emerging players further international exposure before the ODI leg of Zimbabwe's tour.

India Women vs Zimbabwe Women 3rd T20I match details

  • Date: Tuesday, 20 October 2026.
  • Start time: 7:00 PM IST.
  • Venue: Shaheed Veer Narayan Singh International Stadium, Raipur.
  • Series position: India Women lead 2-0.

Shafali and Mandhana set the standard

Shafali Verma produced the decisive innings in the second T20I, striking 55 from 25 balls. Captain Smriti Mandhana added an unbeaten 47 from 25 deliveries as the opening pair put on 96 and India chased 103 in only 8.5 overs.

The emphatic response followed a much tighter first match, when India recovered from 19/4 to win by four wickets. Those contrasting victories have shown both the depth and attacking ceiling of the home side.

Niki Prasad strengthens her case

Niki Prasad has been one of India's most encouraging performers in the series. After making an unbeaten 39 on debut in the first match, she took 2/7 in the second and claimed wickets with her first two deliveries in international cricket. Her composure with both bat and ball gives India another flexible middle-order option.

What Zimbabwe need to improve

Zimbabwe must build longer partnerships and find more powerplay runs. Chipo Mugeri-Tiripano made 34 and captain Josephine Nkomo finished unbeaten on 27 in the second match, but a total of 102/8 left their bowlers with too little margin. The visitors will need their top order to absorb pressure without losing attacking intent.

Will India rotate the playing XI?

With the series secured, India could consider opportunities for squad members who have had limited game time. Any changes should be confirmed only at the toss. The established opening pair and the developing roles of Niki Prasad, Anushka Sharma and India's spin group remain key points to watch.

Probable playing XIs

India Women: Smriti Mandhana (c), Shafali Verma, Anushka Sharma, Harmanpreet Kaur, Niki Prasad, Richa Ghosh (wk), Deepti Sharma, Arundhati Reddy, Kranti Gaud, Sree Charani, Nandni Sharma.

Zimbabwe Women: Modester Mupachikwa (wk), Kelis Ndhlovu, Sharne Mayers, Chipo Mugeri-Tiripano, Josephine Nkomo (c), Beloved Biza, Kudzai Chigora, Loryn Phiri, Nomvelo Sibanda, Nyasha Gwanzura, Lindokuhle Mabhero.

Probable XIs are editorial projections and remain subject to the toss.

India Women's T20I squad

Smriti Mandhana (c), Shafali Verma (vc), Niki Prasad, Harmanpreet Kaur, Anushka Sharma, Richa Ghosh (wk), G Kamalini (wk), Deepti Sharma, Prema Rawat, Sree Charani, Nandni Sharma, Kranti Gaud, Arundhati Reddy, Jintimani Kalita and Minnu Mani.

Prediction

India enter as clear favourites after controlling both matches in different ways. Zimbabwe have shown patches of resistance, but the hosts' deeper batting and broader bowling options make a 3-0 India victory the likelier outcome.
