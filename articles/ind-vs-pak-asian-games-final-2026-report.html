IND vs PAK Final Highlights: India Wins Gold by 19 Runs in a Six-Hitting Thriller

Team India held their nerve against a miraculous 96-run onslaught by H. Nawaz to defend 211 and secure the Asian Games Gold Medal in Nisshin, Japan.

3 October 2026 CricXZ Sports Desk Match Report

CricXZ editorial illustration — India claims the Gold Medal after defending a massive 211-run total against Pakistan.

Japan ke Korogi Sports Park me khele gaye Asian Games 2026 T20 Final mein Team India ne Pakistan ko 19 runs se hara kar Gold Medal par kabza kar liya hai. Ek aisi pitch jahan gend baahar kam aur hawa mein zyada thi, wahan match kisi Hollywood blockbuster se kam nahi tha. Abhishek Sharma ke powerplay sixes aur H. Nawaz ki aatishi 96 runs ki pari ne is match ko fans ke liye behad yaadgaar bana diya.

🏆 Asian Games 2026 - Gold Medal Match

🇮🇳 India: 211/6 (20 Overs)
🇵🇰 Pakistan: 192/8 (20 Overs)
India won by 19 runs & secured the GOLD MEDAL 🥇

Abhishek Sharma ka Powerplay Storm

Toss jeet kar pehle batting karne utri Team India ko jis aatishi shuruat ki zaroorat thi, wo Abhishek Sharma ne di. Unhone maidan ke har kone mein shots khele aur sirf 20 balls mein apni fifty poori kar li. Abhishek ne 28 balls par dhuaandhaar 61 runs banaye, jisme 7 massive sixes aur 3 boundaries shamil the. Inki is hitting ke chalte India ne powerplay mein hi match par pakad bana li thi.

Middle overs mein Pakistan ke star pacer Shaheen Afridi ne wapsi ki koshish ki. Shaheen ne apni lethal yorkers ka istemal karte hue 4 overs mein 35 runs dekar 3 crucial wickets chatkaye, lekin Indian batters ne aggressive approach nahi chhoda aur board par 211/6 ka pahad jaisa total khada kar diya.

H. Nawaz ka Counter-Attack: 96 off 51 Balls

212 runs ke target ka pichha karna final jaise pressure match mein kabhi aasan nahi hota. Pakistan ki shuruat ladkhadayi, lekin phir maidan par H. Nawaz ka toofan aaya. Ek waqt par lag raha tha India match aasaani se jeet jayega, par Nawaz ne akele dum par match ki kahani palat di.

Nawaz ne sirf 51 balls par 96 runs thok diye. Century se sirf 4 runs pehle out hone wale Nawaz ki is aatishi pari mein 5 chauke aur 7 gagan-chumbi chhakke (sixes) shamil the. Unhone spinners aur pacers dono ko target kiya aur Indian dugout ki dhadkanein badha di thi. Asian Games ke final mein aisi fearless batting dekh kar maidan mein baithe darshak bhi dang reh gaye.

Turning Point of the Match

H. Nawaz jab tak crease par the, Pakistan match mein bana hua tha. Aakhiri 3 overs mein pressure badh raha tha aur Jasprit Bumrah ki ek sateek yorker par Nawaz (96) ka wicket girte hi match poori tarah se India ke paksh mein chala gaya.

Death Overs aur India ki Jeet

Aakhiri 3 overs mein Pakistan ko tezi se runs chahiye the. Par Indian pace attack ki world-class death bowling ne pressure banaye rakha. Jaise hi Nawaz out hue, Pakistan ki umeedein wahin khatam ho gayin. Team India ne Pakistan ko 192/8 ke score par rok diya aur 19 runs se aitihasik jeet darj ki.

Is Gold Medal jeet ke sath hi India ne ek baar phir T20 cricket mein apna dabdaba saabit kar diya hai. Jaha ek taraf Abhishek Sharma ki power-hitting India ke kaam aayi, wahi H. Nawaz ki 96 runs ki heroics T20 history mein lambe samay tak yaad rakhi jayegi.
