India Beat West Indies by 8 Wickets as Kohli, Gill Shine in 1st ODI

Virat Kohli scored an unbeaten 139 and Shubman Gill made 110 as India chased 296 with eight wickets in hand in Thiruvananthapuram.

Published 28 September 2026 By CricXZ Sports Desk Match Report

India opened their three-match ODI series against West Indies with an eight-wicket victory at the Sports Hub International Cricket Stadium in Thiruvananthapuram. Chasing 296, India reached 299 for two with 13.4 overs remaining, with Virat Kohli unbeaten on 139 and captain Shubman Gill scoring 110.

The result gave India a 1-0 lead in the series and also produced a major individual milestone for Kohli, who crossed 15,000 ODI runs during his unbeaten match-winning innings.

India vs West Indies first ODI result

  • West Indies: 295/7 in 50 overs
  • India: 299/2 in 36.4 overs
  • Result: India won by 8 wickets
  • Venue: Sports Hub International Cricket Stadium, Thiruvananthapuram
  • Date: 27 September 2026
  • Series: India lead 1-0

West Indies post 295 after Greaves century

West Indies were asked to bat first and built their innings around Justin Greaves, who scored 101. John Campbell added 62 as the visitors established a strong opening platform.

India's bowlers pulled the innings back during the middle overs. Kuldeep Yadav was particularly effective, finishing with 4/40 and removing several important West Indies batters. India eventually restricted the visitors to 295/7 from their 50 overs.

Shubman Gill sets up India's chase

India's chase began positively and Gill took control at the top of the order. The India captain scored 110 from 108 balls, combining measured strokeplay with timely attacking shots.

Gill's century gave India a strong platform and allowed the chase to remain ahead of the required rate. His partnership with Kohli became the defining phase of the innings.

Virat Kohli produces another ODI masterclass

Kohli was at the centre of India's successful chase, finishing unbeaten on 139 from 88 balls. He struck nine sixes and controlled the chase as India moved steadily towards the target.

The century was Kohli's 55th in ODI cricket, extending his record tally of ODI hundreds. His innings also took him beyond the 15,000-run mark in ODI cricket, adding another major milestone to his international career.

Kohli and Gill put India firmly in control

Kohli and Gill added 151 runs for the third wicket, turning a competitive target into a comfortable chase. Gill was eventually dismissed for 110, but India were already in a commanding position.

Ruturaj Gaikwad then joined Kohli and helped complete the chase. Kohli remained unbeaten as India reached 299/2 in only 36.4 overs.

Kuldeep Yadav makes the difference with the ball

Kuldeep's 4/40 was one of India's most important contributions before the chase. His wickets prevented West Indies from converting their strong start into a much larger total.

The left-arm wrist-spinner's control through the middle overs ensured India had a manageable target, allowing the top order to attack during the chase.

India take 1-0 lead in the ODI series

The eight-wicket victory puts India ahead 1-0 in the three-match series. The second ODI is scheduled for 30 September in Guwahati, followed by the third ODI in New Chandigarh on 3 October.

For India, the opening match combined a strong bowling recovery with a dominant top-order chase. For West Indies, the series now moves into a must-win second ODI if they are to keep the contest alive.

Key performances

  • Shubman Gill: 110 from 108 balls
  • Kuldeep Yadav: 4/40
  • Justin Greaves: 101
  • John Campbell: 62
