Blair Tickner Ruled Out of Second India Test; Matt Fisher Called Up

New Zealand have replaced the injured pace bowler in their squad ahead of the Christchurch Test.

24 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskTest Squad Update

AI-generated CricXZ editorial illustration; not an actual match photograph. Artwork illustrates the squad update.

Blair Tickner will miss New Zealand’s second Test against India after injuring his right hamstring during training in Wellington. New Zealand Cricket confirmed the setback on 24 November and called Matt Fisher into the squad for the Christchurch match.

Confirmed Squad Update

Unavailable: Blair Tickner, right-hamstring injury
Replacement: Matt Fisher
Next Test: 27 November–1 December 2026
Venue: Hagley Oval, Christchurch
Selection status: Squad call-up confirmed; Fisher’s place in the playing XI has not been announced in these updates.

What NZC Confirmed About Tickner

NZC said Tickner sustained the injury while training in Wellington during the week. The board’s 24 November statement ruled him out of the second Test and said further assessments in Christchurch would help establish his return-to-play timeline.

No recovery date was given in that statement. The confirmed absence therefore applies to the upcoming India Test; it does not establish how long he will remain unavailable beyond this match.

Fisher Joins the Christchurch Squad

Fisher was due to link up with the group in Christchurch on 24 November. NZC noted that he had already played in the T20I and ODI portions of India’s tour.

The ICC’s accompanying report highlighted his six wickets in the recent ODI series. That gives the call-up some immediate tour context, although white-ball performances alone do not determine Test selection.

Squad Replacement Does Not Confirm the XI

Fisher’s inclusion restores a pace-bowling option to the touring-series squad. It is not an announcement that he will play at Hagley Oval: the final combination remains a separate selection decision.

For readers following India’s own selection discussion, our report on Shubman Gill’s explanation of Mohammed Siraj’s omission also separates a player’s squad status from selection in a particular XI.

Second Test Follows the Wellington Draw

The opening Test at Wellington, played from 19 to 23 November, ended in a draw. The second match is scheduled for 27 November to 1 December in Christchurch, leaving the two-match series level heading into the finale.

Read our Wellington final-day report for the match result and our India WTC qualification explainer for the calculations published after that draw.

CricXZ Analysis: A Change in Available Options

The immediate effect of the injury is on New Zealand’s available bowling choices. Fisher gives the selectors another specialist pace option, but naming a replacement does not reveal the balance they will choose for Christchurch.

The useful next checkpoints are Tickner’s assessment and the confirmed match XI. Until those are announced, a recovery estimate or a guaranteed Fisher appearance would go beyond the information available. This section is CricXZ editorial analysis.
