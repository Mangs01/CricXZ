Vaibhav Sooryavanshi Smashes 204 off 53, Breaks List A Records Against UAE

A 28-ball hundred, a 52-ball double century and 28 sixes defined Sooryavanshi’s remarkable innings in Doha.

24 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskList A Records Report

AI-generated CricXZ editorial illustration; not an actual match photograph. Artwork illustrates the record innings.

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi struck 204 from 53 balls against the UAE in Doha on 24 November 2026, breaking List A batting records as India A won by seven wickets. The 15-year-old reached his century in 28 balls and his double century in 52, finishing with 28 sixes.

India A vs UAE: Match Snapshot

Venue: University of Doha for Science and Technology, Doha
UAE: 249/8 in 50 overs
India A: 254/3 in 14.2 overs
Result: India A won by seven wickets
Sooryavanshi: 204 off 53 balls, seven fours and 28 sixes
Player of the Match: Vaibhav Sooryavanshi.

Three Records in One Innings

The ICC reported that Sooryavanshi’s hundred took just 28 deliveries, one fewer than Jake Fraser-McGurk’s 29-ball List A century for South Australia against Tasmania in 2023. He then brought up 200 in 52 balls, surpassing Chad Bowes’ 103-ball double century for Canterbury against Otago in 2024.

His six-hitting also set a new List A mark. The 28 sixes exceeded D’Arcy Short’s previous total of 23 in an innings for Western Australia against Queensland in 2018.

52-Ball Double Century, 53-Ball Innings

The milestone and the completed innings are different figures. Sooryavanshi reached 200 from 52 balls, then finished on 204 from 53. Calling the whole innings “204 off 52” would incorrectly combine them.

His calculated strike rate was 384.91, using 204 ÷ 53 × 100 and rounding to two decimal places. The seven fours and 28 sixes contributed 196 runs in boundaries; the remaining eight came through running between the wickets. These are CricXZ calculations from the scorecard figures.

India A Finish the Chase in 14.2 Overs

India A were chasing 250 after the UAE made 249/8. Harpreet Singh Bhatia scored 130 from 144 balls, while Arshad Khan returned 4/43 for India A.

Sooryavanshi dominated the response before being dismissed with India A on 245/3. Rinku Singh finished unbeaten on 27 and sealed the chase with a six, taking the total to 254/3 in 14.2 overs.

In cricket notation, 14.2 overs means 14 completed overs plus two balls: 86 legal deliveries, rather than a decimal fraction of an over.

These Are List A Records, Not Senior ODI Records

The competition is classified as List A one-day cricket. India A is distinct from India’s senior international ODI team, so the achievement should be described in the List A category. A record in that broader category does not become a senior ODI record simply because the opponent is the UAE.
