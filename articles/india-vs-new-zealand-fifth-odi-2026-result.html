Arshdeep Stars as India Defend 209 to Seal New Zealand ODI Series 3-1

India beat New Zealand by 22 runs at Bay Oval to win the ODI series 3-1. Arshdeep took 3/30 after Axar Patel and Ishan Kishan scored 46 each.

Published 15 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskMatch Report • Mount Maunganui

India defended 209 to beat New Zealand by 22 runs in the fifth ODI at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, on 15 November 2026, securing the five-match series 3-1. Arshdeep Singh led the attack with 3/30 as the hosts were dismissed for 187.

Fifth ODI: Final Score

  • India: 209 all out in 42.1 overs.
  • New Zealand: 187 all out in 48.1 overs; target 210.
  • Result: India won by 22 runs.
  • Series: India won 3-1.
  • Player of the Match: Arshdeep Singh, 3/30 and 14 runs.

Axar and Kishan Give India Something to Defend

New Zealand elected to field and reduced India to 34/3. Axar Patel and Ishan Kishan each made 46, providing the most substantial contributions in an innings that never developed into a large total.

Shreyas Iyer added 22 and Naman Dhir also scored 22. Jacob Duffy took 3/60 and Mitchell Santner returned 3/47, keeping the pressure on as India lost wickets through the middle and latter stages.

At 183/9, India still needed something from the final pair. Arshdeep and Mohammed Siraj added 26 for the last wicket, lifting the total to 209. Those late runs became valuable protection for the bowlers.

Arshdeep Leads a Collective Bowling Response

India could not rely on scoreboard pressure alone with New Zealand chasing 210. Arshdeep conceded just 30 runs from his ten overs and claimed three wickets, including Will Young and Nick Kelly. Siraj removed Tim Seifert and Finn Allen and finished with 2/50.

New Zealand reached 64/1, but the chase lost its footing as wickets interrupted the partnerships. Dean Foxcroft top-scored with 31; no home batter reached a half-century.

Auqib Nabi, Yash Thakur and Naman Dhir took one wicket apiece. Axar completed the job with 2/28, dismissing Jacob Duffy and then having Blair Tickner stumped to end the innings in the 49th over.

From Fourth-ODI Setback to Series Victory

The result delivered an immediate response to India's 244-run defeat at the same venue in the fourth ODI. That loss had left the series at 2-1 and given New Zealand a chance to draw it. India instead finished with three victories, one defeat and the Hamilton no-result.

The final therefore settled the series outright. A 3-1 finish describes the completed five-match schedule; it does not mean four matches were scheduled.

CricXZ Analysis: Late Runs and Shared Responsibility

The last-wicket partnership and the spread of wickets tell the central story of this defence. India's batting remained vulnerable, but the tail extended the innings beyond 200 and all six bowlers used took a wicket. That gave the captain several ways to disrupt the chase rather than depending on one spell alone.

A series victory should not hide the batting questions raised by the final two matches. This was a successful recovery with the ball, while the top order still has work to do. That distinction is more useful than treating the win as evidence that every part of the side performed equally well.
