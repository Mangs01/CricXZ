Bhuvneshwar Kumar Returns to India T20I Squad After Four Years

The experienced swing bowler has been recalled for India's five-match T20I tour of New Zealand, bringing a proven new-ball option back into Shreyas Iyer's squad.

8 October 2026CricXZ Sports DeskSquad News

CricXZ editorial illustration of Bhuvneshwar Kumar's India T20I comeback for the New Zealand tour.

Bhuvneshwar Kumar has returned to India's T20I squad for the five-match tour of New Zealand beginning on 22 October 2026. The BCCI confirmed his selection on 6 October when it announced the India and India A touring groups.

India's New Zealand T20I Tour

Series: Five T20Is
Dates: 22 October–1 November 2026
Captain: Shreyas Iyer
Vice-captain: Tilak Varma
Comeback: Bhuvneshwar Kumar

Back in India Colours After Nearly Four Years

Bhuvneshwar's previous T20I appearance came against New Zealand at McLean Park in Napier on 22 November 2022. His selection for the 2026 tour therefore ends an absence of almost four years from India's international side.

The return is especially notable because the series takes place in New Zealand, where conditions can reward bowlers capable of controlling swing and seam with the new ball. Selection guarantees a place in the touring squad, although his inclusion in each playing XI will depend on team balance and match conditions.

Why Bhuvneshwar's Skill Set Still Matters

Bhuvneshwar built his international reputation through late movement, disciplined lines and the ability to bowl both in the powerplay and at the death. According to his ICC career profile, he has taken 90 wickets in 87 T20Is, alongside 141 ODI wickets and 63 Test wickets.

India's squad also includes Arshdeep Singh, Mayank Yadav and Prince Yadav, giving the selectors different pace profiles. Bhuvneshwar offers experience and new-ball control within that group, while spinners Varun Chakaravarthy, Kuldeep Yadav and Axar Patel provide contrasting options.

Shreyas Iyer Continues as T20I Captain

Shreyas Iyer will captain India in New Zealand, with Tilak Varma serving as vice-captain. The touring squad retains much of the group involved in the ongoing West Indies series, including Abhishek Sharma, Sanju Samson, Ishan Kishan, Shivam Dube and Naman Dhir.

The combination gives India explosive batting, multiple spin choices and a varied pace attack. Bhuvneshwar's role could be to provide control around the younger quicks, especially during the first six overs.

Five-Match T20I Schedule

  • 1st T20I: 22 October — Christchurch
  • 2nd T20I: 24 October — Christchurch
  • 3rd T20I: 27 October — Wellington
  • 4th T20I: 30 October — Auckland
  • 5th T20I: 1 November — Hamilton

India's T20I Squad for New Zealand

Shreyas Iyer (c), Abhishek Sharma, Sanju Samson (wk), Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Ishan Kishan (wk), Tilak Varma (vc), Axar Patel, Shivam Dube, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Naman Dhir, Prince Yadav, Mayank Yadav, Arshdeep Singh, Bhuvneshwar Kumar, Varun Chakaravarthy and Kuldeep Yadav.

The BCCI stated that the ODI and Test squads would be announced later. It separately named an India A squad, captained by Dhruv Jurel, for three multi-day matches against New Zealand A.

From Hong Kong Sixes Captain to India Recall

The comeback follows another leadership assignment for the experienced seamer. Bhuvneshwar was recently named captain of India's squad for the Hong Kong Sixes 2026, adding a useful contextual link between his recent role and his return to the senior national squad.

The two selections are separate events, but together they underline his continued relevance in short-format cricket. His performances and precise role in New Zealand will now determine whether the recall develops into a sustained international return.

What the Recall Means

The decision gives India an experienced swing bowler for a challenging away series without reducing the team's investment in younger pace options. It also creates competition for places: Bhuvneshwar must earn his spot in the XI alongside Arshdeep, Mayank and Prince.

For Bhuvneshwar, the first T20I in Christchurch could mark his first India appearance since the 2022 Napier match. Until the playing XI is announced, that remains a possibility rather than a certainty.
