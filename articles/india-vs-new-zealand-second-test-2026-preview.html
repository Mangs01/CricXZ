India vs New Zealand 2nd Test Preview: Morkel Calls for New-Ball Impact

Morne Morkel wants India to make the new ball count as the series moves to Hagley Oval.

26 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskSecond Test Preview

AI-generated CricXZ editorial illustration; not an actual match photograph. Artwork illustrates the upcoming Test.

India and New Zealand head to Hagley Oval, Christchurch, for the second and final Test with the series level after the Wellington draw. The match is scheduled for 27 November to 1 December 2026, with the opening day starting at 3:30 am IST.

Match Details

Fixture: New Zealand vs India, 2nd Test
Venue: Hagley Oval, Christchurch
Dates: 27 November–1 December 2026
Scheduled start: 11:00 am NZDT / 3:30 am IST on 27 November
Series: Level 0–0
Status: Pre-match preview; playing XIs and toss not announced here.

Morkel Wants an Attacking New-Ball Approach

India bowling coach Morne Morkel’s central message was to use the harder Kookaburra ball aggressively. He explained that bowlers may need to accept some runs while seeking wickets before the ball softens and the surface becomes less responsive.

Morkel described the Christchurch pitch as green but cautioned that appearances did not guarantee its behaviour. His expectation is a pre-match assessment, not evidence of how the match will unfold.

Siraj, Kamboj and Brar Remain Selection Talking Points

Morkel backed Mohammed Siraj’s quality, defended Anshul Kamboj’s contribution in Wellington and praised Gurnoor Brar’s readiness. Those comments support the available options without announcing a change to India’s XI.

Our earlier Siraj selection report explains the first-Test decision. It does not establish who will play in Christchurch.

New Zealand’s Squad Has an Injury Replacement

NZC has ruled Blair Tickner out with a right-hamstring injury and called Matt Fisher into the squad. Fisher’s inclusion is a squad replacement, not confirmation of a place in the match XI.

Read the Tickner–Fisher update for the dated official injury announcement.

What the Wellington Draw Leaves to Resolve

The first Test ended with New Zealand on 239/5 in their second innings after India’s 720/9 declared. Neither side therefore carries a series lead into the finale.

Our completed Wellington report records that result. The Christchurch match will be covered separately once play begins; this page remains a preview.

CricXZ Preview: Three Phases to Watch

  • The first new-ball spell: Watch the balance between fuller attacking deliveries and the control needed to avoid easy scoring opportunities.
  • Batting through pressure: Early movement can demand patience, but the value of an opening partnership also depends on how conditions develop.
  • The older ball: If the surface slows, changes of angle, disciplined fields and sustained accuracy may become more important.

These are CricXZ editorial observations, not predictions of wickets, scores or a winner. A green-looking pitch alone cannot settle a selection debate or establish that the match will favour one team.

Start-Time Clarification for Indian Readers

The listed start of 22:00 GMT on 26 November is 11:00 am in Christchurch and 3:30 am in India on 27 November. The different calendar dates reflect time zones, not a different fixture.

Match times remain subject to official changes. This preview does not provide an independently verified broadcast schedule or weather forecast.
