IND vs WI 3rd ODI Highlights: Shai Hope's 162 Trumps KL Rahul's 129 in Epic Chase

West Indies avoided a series whitewash with a historic 352-run chase. Captain Shai Hope's masterclass of 162 overshadowed KL Rahul's brilliant century, while Shubman Gill was crowned Player of the Series.

4 October 2026 CricXZ Sports Desk Match Report

CricXZ editorial illustration — Shai Hope led the West Indies to a historic 5-wicket victory in the final ODI.

In a high-scoring thriller, the West Indies defeated India by 5 wickets in the third and final ODI. Although Team India had already secured the series 2-1 following a historic 406-run chase powered by Shubman Gill's 223* in the previous match, West Indies captain Shai Hope ensured the hosts avoided a clean sweep by successfully chasing down a massive target of 352 runs.

🏏 India vs West Indies - 3rd ODI

🇮🇳 India: 351/7 (50 Overs)
🌴 West Indies: 352/5 (48.2 Overs)
West Indies won by 5 wkts (India wins series 2-1) 🏆

KL Rahul's 129-Run Masterclass

Batting first, India posted a formidable total of 351/7 in their allotted 50 overs, largely thanks to a spectacular innings from KL Rahul. Stepping up when the team needed acceleration, Rahul smashed a breathtaking 129 runs off just 87 deliveries. His knock was a perfect blend of elegant cover drives and towering sixes, setting what initially looked like a match-winning target.

Player of the Match: Shai Hope's Historic 162

Chasing 352 is never easy, but the West Indies found their savior in captain Shai Hope. Deservedly awarded the Player of the Match, Hope delivered a phenomenal knock of 162 runs off 143 balls. Playing the ultimate anchor role, he effectively neutralized the Indian spin attack, constantly rotated the strike, and punished loose deliveries to orchestrate one of the finest run chases in recent ODI history. His masterful century guided the hosts across the line in 48.2 overs.

Player of the Series: Shubman Gill

Despite the loss in the final game, India's dominant series victory was headlined by the unstoppable Shubman Gill. He was rightfully named the Player of the Series for his consistent heroics, most notably his record-breaking, unbeaten 223* in the monumental 406-run chase during the 2nd ODI, which had initially set India on course for a potential 3-0 whitewash.

Conclusion

This match will go down in cricket history as a true battle of epic centuries. While KL Rahul's explosive form remains a massive positive for India heading into future tournaments, Shai Hope and the West Indies proved that on their day, their batting lineup possesses the firepower and resilience to chase down any total on the global stage.
