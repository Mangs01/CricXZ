Vaibhav Sooryavanshi Becomes Youngest Captain in Men's T20 Cricket at 15

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi led Bihar aged 15 years and 232 days in the SMAT opener against Saurashtra. Read the captaincy record and confirmed match result.

Published 14 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskDomestic Cricket

AI-generated CricXZ editorial illustration in Bihar-inspired colours; not an actual match or official kit photograph.

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi became the youngest captain in men's T20 cricket when he led Bihar against Saurashtra in the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy at Holkar Stadium, Indore, on 14 November 2026. He was 15 years and 232 days old on the day of the match.

A Captaincy Record at 15

Rajasthan Royals, Sooryavanshi's IPL franchise, confirmed the milestone in its official report. He moved ahead of Croatia's Zach Vukusic, who had captained his national side at 17 years and 311 days.

The comparison is across men's T20 cricket, including recognised domestic matches. Sooryavanshi was leading Bihar in a domestic competition; the milestone does not mean that he has been appointed India's T20I captain.

There is also an Indian benchmark: Mahipal Lomror was 18 years and 67 days old when he captained Rajasthan in the 2018 Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy, according to the Royals' record summary.

How His First Match as Bihar Captain Went

  • Date: 14 November 2026.
  • Venue: Holkar Stadium, Indore.
  • Bihar: 137/9 in 20 overs.
  • Saurashtra: 138/5 in 16.3 overs.
  • Result: Saurashtra won by five wickets, with 21 balls remaining.
  • Sooryavanshi: 18 from 12 balls, caught and bowled by Chetan Sakariya.

Bihar chose to bat after winning the toss. Sooryavanshi's innings contained two fours and a six, giving him a strike rate of 150.00, but his departure left Bihar at 35/2 in the fifth over.

Saurashtra completed the chase with five wickets intact. The captaincy record therefore sits alongside a defeat on his debut as Bihar's senior T20 captain, rather than a winning start.

The Appointment Covers Two Matches

Sooryavanshi was named Bihar captain for the opening two fixtures, against Saurashtra on 14 November and Railways on 16 November. Suraj Kashyap was appointed vice-captain.

The scope matters: this is an announced two-match assignment, not confirmation that he will lead Bihar throughout the tournament. Rajasthan Royals' earlier squad announcement sets out that limited appointment.

What the Leadership Opportunity Adds

CricXZ analysis: Opening the batting and captaining a side ask different questions of a young player. Batting focuses attention on the next delivery; leadership also involves field placements, bowling changes and helping teammates respond to pressure.

One match cannot establish how well he will manage those responsibilities over time. The useful next step is to follow how he adapts across the assignment, while keeping his age record separate from judgments about Bihar's performance.

A New Milestone in His Domestic Career

The appointment adds a leadership milestone to Sooryavanshi's progression through domestic, franchise and international cricket.
