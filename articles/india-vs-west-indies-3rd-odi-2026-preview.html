India vs West Indies 3rd ODI: India Eye Series Clean Sweep After Record Chase

Following a historic 406-run chase powered by Shubman Gill's monumental double century in the 2nd ODI, an unstoppable Team India aims for a 3-0 whitewash against the West Indies.

October 2026 CricXZ Sports Desk Match Preview

CricXZ editorial illustration — Team India looks to complete a series sweep in the 3rd ODI.

Confidence is sky-high in the Indian dressing room as they prepare for the third and final ODI against the West Indies. After an unbelievable performance in the second match, where India successfully hunted down a massive target of 406 in just 43.3 overs, the hosts are now eyeing a ruthless 3-0 series clean sweep.

Match Details and Current Status

  • Status: Upcoming (3rd ODI)
  • Series position: India lead the 3-match series 2–0
  • Significance: India looking for a whitewash; West Indies playing for pride.

India's Unstoppable Momentum

The storyline of the series has undoubtedly been the phenomenal form of Shubman Gill. His breathtaking 223 not out in the previous game shattered multiple records and demoralized the Caribbean bowling attack. With the series already in the bag, the Indian team management might look to test their bench strength, potentially giving opportunities to rising stars and resting a few key pacers.

However, the core philosophy remains unchanged: aggressive, fearless cricket. Whether chasing or setting a target, the Indian top order looks virtually impenetrable on current form.

Focus Area for Team India

Bowling discipline: While the batters achieved a miraculous chase in the 2nd ODI, conceding 400+ runs is a slight concern. The bowling unit, particularly the death bowlers, will be eager to tighten things up and deliver a clinical performance in the series finale.

West Indies: Pride at Stake

For the West Indies, the 2nd ODI was a heartbreaker. Scoring over 400 runs in an ODI and still losing the match by 8 wickets is a tough pill to swallow. Their batters have shown they can handle the Indian conditions, but their bowling attack desperately needs to find a way to break partnerships and contain the run flow.

To avoid a whitewash, the visitors will need early wickets in the powerplay. If they allow the Indian openers to settle, another long day in the field awaits the Caribbean side.

Predicted Playing XIs (Subject to Toss)

India Probable XI: Shubman Gill (c), Yashasvi Jaiswal, Ruturaj Gaikwad, Shreyas Iyer, KL Rahul (wk), Rinku Singh, Washington Sundar, Axar Patel, Ravi Bishnoi, Mohammed Siraj, Mukesh Kumar. (India may rest Jasprit Bumrah/Hardik Pandya)

West Indies Probable XI: Shai Hope (c & wk), Brandon King, Alick Athanaze, Keacy Carty, Nicholas Pooran, Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Gudakesh Motie, Alzarri Joseph, Shamar Joseph, Jayden Seales.
