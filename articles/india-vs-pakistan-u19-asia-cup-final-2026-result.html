India vs Pakistan U19 Asia Cup Final: Ulva Seals Last-Ball One-Wicket Win

Mohit Ulva hit the last-ball boundary as India chased 338 to beat Pakistan by one wicket in the U19 Asia Cup final. Read the verified scores and key partnerships.

India won the Under-19 Asia Cup with a one-wicket victory over Pakistan on the final ball at Al Amerat, Oman, on 14 November 2026. Chasing 338, India finished on 339/9 after Mohit Ulva struck the winning boundary. His unbeaten 49 from 26 balls completed a recovery that had looked unlikely when India lost their eighth wicket.

India vs Pakistan U19 Final: Result Summary

  • Pakistan U19: 337/9 in 50 overs.

  • Result: India won by one wicket, on the final ball.
  • Venue and match date: Al Amerat, Oman; 14 November 2026.
  • Player of the Match: Mohit Ulva, 49 not out from 26 balls.
  • Player of the Tournament: Aaryavir Sehwag, 361 runs.

Ulva Holds His Nerve in the Final Over

India needed eight from Ahmed Hussain's last over with one wicket remaining. A dot ball, a wide and four singles left Ulva needing three from the final delivery. He advanced and sent the ball towards wide long-off, where it bounced before reaching the boundary.

Chigurupati Venkata finished unbeaten on 11 from eight balls. The last pair added 30, keeping the target within reach after Manal Chauhan's dismissal. Ulva was the batter who finished the chase, but Venkata's contribution ensured he did not have to do it alone.

The Recovery from 252/8

India's position became particularly difficult at 252/8 after 41.3 overs. Chauhan and Ulva then added 57 for the ninth wicket. Chauhan made 47 from 32 balls before departing at 309/9 in the 48th over.

CricXZ analysis: That partnership changed the task for the final pair. The chase still demanded scoring under pressure, but the earlier counterattack had reduced the distance to the target. A match report focused only on the final boundary would miss the stand that made the finish possible.

Raichandani and Sehwag Build the Chase

Captain Lakshya Raichandani contributed 80 and Aaryavir Sehwag made 58. Their opening partnership was worth 95, giving India a foundation against a demanding target. The innings later lost momentum through a sequence of wickets, leaving the lower order to rebuild.

Sehwag also received the tournament award after finishing with 361 runs. India's victory brought their ninth Under-19 Asia Cup title and their first since 2021, according to Reuters.

Usman's Century Takes Pakistan to 337

Pakistan chose to bat and built their total around Usman Khan's 107. Abdul Qadir added 80, while captain Farhan Yousaf scored 63. Their contributions gave Pakistan enough runs to keep the final competitive until the last delivery.

India's leading wicket-takers were Kavya Patel with 4/68 and Venkata with 3/40. Rizwanullah's 3/53 and Niqab Shafiq's 2/57 helped Pakistan take the chase deep, but the late partnerships denied them the title.

Why This Was More Than a Last-Ball Shot

CricXZ analysis: The final combined a substantial first-innings total, a productive opening stand and a lower-order rescue. Neither side controlled every stage. India's decisive advantage was that their last two partnerships continued finding runs while Pakistan needed only one more wicket.
