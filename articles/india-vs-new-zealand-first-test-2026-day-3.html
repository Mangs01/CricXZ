India vs New Zealand 1st Test Day 3: Rahul 136 Powers 118-Run Lead

Rahul's century anchored India's reply before Gill and Pant pushed the tourists into a 118-run lead at the Basin Reserve.

21 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskDay 3 Stumps Report

AI-generated CricXZ editorial illustration; not an actual match photograph. Scores show Day 3 stumps.

India closed Day 3 of the first Test against New Zealand on 486/4 in Wellington on 21 November 2026, a first-innings lead of 118. KL Rahul made 136, Shubman Gill fell for 98 and Rishabh Pant was unbeaten on 55. New Zealand had been dismissed for 368.

Wellington Test: Day 3 Snapshot

Venue: Cello Basin Reserve, Wellington
New Zealand: 368 all out (121 overs)
India: 486/4 (125 overs)
Lead: India ahead by 118 runs
Overnight batters: Rishabh Pant 55*, Anshul Kamboj 0*
Status: Stumps, Day 3. Match ongoing; no result yet.

Rahul Gives India's Reply a Solid Base

KL Rahul faced 290 balls for his 136, hitting nine fours and two sixes before Nathan Smith bowled him. His innings gave India a substantial contribution from the opening position after Yashasvi Jaiswal's 85 from 89 balls.

Devdutt Padikkal added 80 from 175 deliveries before falling to Kyle Jamieson. Those contributions meant India were able to build their response across the top order rather than depend on a single innings.

Gill Falls Two Short; Pant Finishes Unbeaten

Gill scored 98 from 146 balls but was bowled by Jacob Duffy in the final over of the day. Pant finished on 55 not out from 62 balls, with five fours and two sixes, while Kamboj survived two deliveries without scoring.

According to the ICC's Day 3 report, this was the first occasion on which India's top five batters had all reached fifty in an away Test. The ICC also identified Rahul's innings as his 13th Test century.

How the Match Reached This Point

India resumed on 159/1 after Day 2 and added 327 while losing three wickets. New Zealand's first innings had been built around Henry Nicholls' unbeaten 175 and Glenn Phillips' 118. Jasprit Bumrah took 4/92 as the hosts finished with 368.

CricXZ Analysis: The Lead Is a Platform

India's advantage is meaningful, but it does not settle the Test. A first-innings lead reduces the runs New Zealand can use to set a target, yet India still need to turn their position into enough time and pressure to take another ten wickets.

Pant's presence gives the next session a clear point of interest. More runs could strengthen India's control; quick wickets would give New Zealand a route back into the contest. The balance between extending the lead and leaving time to bowl will matter as much as the headline total. This is CricXZ editorial analysis, not a prediction of the result.

What to Follow on Day 4

The immediate questions are how far India can extend their innings and how New Zealand respond when they bat again. This report records the Day 3 close only. Later scores, declarations and any final result require a fresh match update.
