Australia Beat Zimbabwe by 1 Wicket as Inglis and Ellis Seal 3–0 Sweep

20 September 2026 · Harare Sports Club · CricXZ News

Australia survived a final-over thriller against Zimbabwe, winning the third ODI by one wicket with one ball to spare. Josh Inglis made 103, while Nathan Ellis followed his 3/50 with an unbeaten 36 to complete a 3–0 series sweep in Harare.

Zimbabwe 271/850 oversAustralia 272/949.5 oversResult Australia won by 1 wicketSeries: Australia 3–0

Zimbabwe set Australia 272

Sikandar Raza anchored Zimbabwe's innings with 81 not out. Craig Ervine added 59 and Innocent Kaia made 55 before retiring hurt. Zimbabwe reached 271/8 from their 50 overs; Ellis returned 3/50 to restrict the hosts late in the innings.

Inglis holds the chase together

Australia lost both openers early, but Inglis rebuilt the chase with a 103 from 102 balls. He struck 11 fours and shared an 85-run partnership with Alex Carey, who made 34. Brad Evans took 4/47 for Zimbabwe, and the contest swung again when Inglis fell with 45 runs still required.

Ellis finishes a last-wicket thriller

Ellis stayed unbeaten on 36 from 44 balls. Australia needed nine runs from the final over; he struck a six off its first delivery, then completed the chase with a single off the fifth ball. Spencer Johnson was unbeaten on seven as Australia finished 272/9 in 49.5 overs.

Key numbers

  • Josh Inglis: 103 from 102 balls.
  • Nathan Ellis: 36 not out and 3/50.
  • Sikandar Raza: 81 not out for Zimbabwe.

  • Australia won the three-match ODI series 3–0.
