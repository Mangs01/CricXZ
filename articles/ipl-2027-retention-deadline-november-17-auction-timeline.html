IPL 2027 Retention Deadline: Reported November 17 Date and Auction Timeline

IPL 2027 retention lists are reportedly due on 17 November, with the mini-auction planned for 16 December in Goa. Read the timeline and what is confirmed.

Published 14 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskIPL Retention Guide

AI-generated CricXZ editorial illustration; not an actual auction photograph. Dates shown are reported.

IPL franchises reportedly have until 17 November 2026 to submit their retention and release decisions for the 2027 season. The mini-auction is reported for 16 December in Goa. This guide, checked on 14 November, separates the reported timetable from public IPL announcements and explains what supporters should look for next.

What Is the Latest Retention Deadline?

Cricbuzz's Vijay Tagore reported on 2 November that the BCCI had moved the submission deadline from 15 to 17 November. ANI, in a report carried by The Tribune, also reported the revised date. Both describe communication to franchises; the dates below are attributed reporting, not a public IPL notice independently obtained by CricXZ.

The extension was linked in the reporting to the India-New Zealand fifth ODI on 15 November. It is a change to the squad-decision deadline, rather than a change to when the IPL season begins.

IPL 2027: The Reported Auction Timeline

  • 17 November 2026: retention and release submissions.
  • 23 November: player registration closes.
  • 1 December: franchises submit player choices for the auction.
  • 3 December: final auction register and player sets.
  • 16 December: mini-auction in Goa.

Timeline source: Cricbuzz's report on the BCCI communication. These are reported dates, checked on 14 November; a subsequent official notice can supersede them.

Retention, Release and Trade: Three Different Decisions

Retention means keeping a player in a franchise's squad for the next season. A release makes a place available rather than confirming that player's destination. A trade moves a contracted player between teams through the league's transfer process. An announcement in one category should not be read as a complete squad announcement.

The distinction matters when following social-media lists. A forecast of who a team might keep is an opinion; a final list needs an announcement from the league or the franchise. A released player is also not automatically guaranteed a place in the final auction register or a buyer.

One Trade That Is Already Official

The IPL's 11 November advisory confirms Hardik Pandya's move from Mumbai Indians to Punjab Kings and Arshdeep Singh's move in the opposite direction. That is an announced transaction, unlike a prediction about future retentions. Read our Hardik-Arshdeep trade explainer for the terms and team-balance questions.

Why Last Season's Lists Are Not This Season's Lists

The public IPL release titled TATA IPL 2026 Player Retentions Announced is dated 15 November 2025. It covers the 2026 season and an Abu Dhabi auction. It cannot establish the retained players, auction location or available purses for IPL 2027.

Watch the season in the headline and the announcement date together. A familiar player name or an official-looking table does not make an old list current. This article does not publish a speculative ten-team retained-player list.

Do the Six-Retention Rules Apply Here?

The IPL's September 2024 regulations for the 2025-27 cycle include a six-player retention/Right to Match framework and describe the 2025 auction purse. Copying those provisions into a 2027 mini-auction guide as a fresh six-player limit would confuse different stages of squad building. This article does not apply that limit to the forthcoming window.

The same official advisory distinguishes the auction purse from the broader salary cap, which includes other payments. Its total salary-cap figure should not be labelled the money every franchise can spend in the auction. Current team-by-team balances require current official figures.

What Fans Should Check Next

CricXZ analysis: When the lists appear, first check which roles a team has kept, not just which famous names remain. Opening options, wicketkeeping cover, finishers and bowling across the powerplay and death overs shape the gaps an auction must fill.

Then compare those gaps with the team's officially published squad places and budget. A large available balance alone does not show whether a squad is stronger. Equally, a released player may reflect a tactical or financial decision rather than a complete judgement of ability.

For reliable updates, use the latest dated IPL or franchise release for squad decisions and the final auction register for the player pool. An auction start time, streaming arrangement or team budget is not supplied here without verified current details.
