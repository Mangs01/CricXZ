India vs New Zealand 1st Test Drawn as Mitchell and Phillips Deny Visitors

Mitchell and Phillips held out as New Zealand survived 120 overs in their second innings to leave the two-Test series level.

23 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskFinal Match Report

AI-generated CricXZ editorial illustration; not an actual match photograph. Scores show the final match position.

New Zealand secured a draw against India in the first Test at the Basin Reserve on 23 November 2026, closing their second innings on 239/5. India had declared on 720/9 in reply to 368, but the hosts survived 120 overs to deny the visitors victory in Wellington.

Wellington Test: Final Score

New Zealand: 368 & 239/5 (120 overs)
India: 720/9 declared
Result: Match drawn
Unbeaten at the close: Daryl Mitchell 49*, Glenn Phillips 26*
Series: Level 0-0, with one Test remaining.

India Find Openings, but New Zealand Hold On

New Zealand began the final day on 119/2, still 233 runs behind. India needed eight wickets, and the morning brought encouragement: Washington Sundar removed Devon Conway for 65 before Prasidh Krishna dismissed Rachin Ravindra for 37.

Anshul Kamboj later accounted for Tom Blundell, who made 15. Those breakthroughs took India closer, but they proved to be the only three wickets to fall during the day.

Mitchell and Phillips Make Time the Priority

Mitchell and Phillips stayed together for 43 overs. Mitchell finished unbeaten on 49 from 186 balls, with Phillips on 26 not out from 126. Their resistance kept five wickets intact when the teams agreed to a draw.

New Zealand were still 113 runs behind at the finish. They did not need to erase the deficit to save the Test; protecting their remaining wickets until the contest ended was enough.

A Huge Indian Total Without the Final Reward

India's batting had established a commanding position. KL Rahul's 136 and Rishabh Pant's 128 were central to the 720/9 declaration, which produced a first-innings advantage of 352 runs. New Zealand's first innings had included Henry Nicholls' unbeaten 175 and Phillips' 118.

WTC Impact: India Fourth, New Zealand Third

In its post-match update, the ICC placed India fourth in the World Test Championship standings with a points percentage of 50.00. New Zealand dropped to third on 66.67, allowing Australia to move into second on 69.23.

These figures describe the standings reported after the Wellington draw on 23 November, rather than a live table. The result leaves both teams needing to improve their position in the remaining fixtures; it does not by itself settle qualification.

CricXZ Analysis: The Lead Was Only Half the Job

A large first-innings lead gives a team room to attack, but a Test win still requires the necessary wickets. Here, India's batting created that opportunity and New Zealand's final partnership prevented its conversion.

The important distinction for the next match is between building scoreboard pressure and finding ways to dismiss set batters. New Zealand showed the value of adapting their approach to the time remaining. India will need to assess whether the conditions in Christchurch call for a different bowling balance. Second Test Starts on 27 November

The second and final Test is scheduled for 27 November to 1 December in Christchurch. With the series at 0-0, a win there would decide the series. Team combinations should be assessed against fresh squad and pitch updates nearer the match.
