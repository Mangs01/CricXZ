South Africa vs Bangladesh 1st Test: Confirmed Proteas XI, Start Time and Key Battles

South Africa name Hamza and Paterson for the Wanderers Test against Bangladesh. Read the confirmed XI, Bangladesh squad and 1:30 PM IST start on 15 November.

Published 15 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskTest Preview • Johannesburg

South Africa have announced their XI for the first Test against Bangladesh at the Wanderers in Johannesburg, with Zubayr Hamza and Dane Paterson returning to the side. The two-match World Test Championship series is scheduled to begin on 15 November 2026 at 10:00 local time, or 1:30 PM IST. This preview covers the announced home XI and the main selection and tactical questions before the toss.

South Africa vs Bangladesh 1st Test: Match Details

  • Match: First Test of a two-match series.
  • Scheduled dates: 15–19 November 2026.
  • Venue: DP World Wanderers Stadium, Johannesburg.
  • Scheduled start: 10:00 SAST / 08:00 UTC / 1:30 PM IST.
  • Competition: ICC World Test Championship 2025–27.
  • Second Test: 23–27 November at SuperSport Park, Centurion.

Pre-match status: At the preparation check on 15 November, the match centre listed the game as yet to start. South Africa's XI had been announced; Bangladesh's final XI and the toss decision were still awaited. This is a dated preview, not live-score coverage.

South Africa's Confirmed Playing XI

Aiden Markram, Marques Ackerman, Zubayr Hamza, Temba Bavuma (captain), Matthew Breetzke, Ryan Rickelton (wicketkeeper), Wiaan Mulder, Marco Jansen, Simon Harmer, Kagiso Rabada and Dane Paterson.

The ICC's 14 November announcement identifies Hamza for the No. 3 role and Paterson as the returning seamer. Harmer is the specialist spinner in the announced side. Players named in the wider tour squad should not be added to this XI unless a subsequent team update changes it.

What the Hamza and Paterson Selections Change

CricXZ analysis: Hamza's place at No. 3 puts him between the opening partnership and Bavuma's middle order. The role can require two different responses: rebuilding after an early wicket or extending a platform when the openers have survived the new ball. How he handles that transition will be a useful measure of South Africa's batting structure.

Paterson adds another seam option alongside Rabada, Jansen and Mulder. That gives Bavuma choices over who opens the bowling, who follows the first spell and how to maintain pressure when the ball gets older. The announced combination also leaves a clear role for Harmer rather than relying entirely on pace.

Bangladesh: Confirmed Squad, Final XI Awaited

Bangladesh's squad, as published by the ICC, is: Najmul Hossain Shanto (captain), Tanzid Hasan, Naim Sheikh, Mominul Haque, Mushfiqur Rahim, Litton Das, Towhid Hridoy, Jaker Ali, Mehidy Hasan Miraz, Taijul Islam, Taskin Ahmed, Hasan Mahmud, Shoriful Islam, Ebadot Hossain, Khaled Ahmed and Mahfijul Islam.

Mahfijul is the uncapped batting addition. Naim and Khaled return to the group. A squad announcement establishes availability for selection; it does not confirm that all three will play. We have therefore listed the squad rather than presenting a predicted Bangladesh team as official.

Three Contests That Could Shape the Test

CricXZ analysis — Bangladesh's top order against the new ball: The first challenge is to build partnerships without allowing pressure to produce a cluster of wickets. Leaving accurately and defending the stumps matter alongside finding scoring opportunities. Surviving a spell has more value when the next batter does not immediately have to restart the innings.

CricXZ analysis — Bangladesh's seamers against South Africa's top three: Taskin, Hasan and the other pace options in the squad offer Bangladesh different combinations. Once the XI is known, watch whether they can keep a consistent line through changes of bowler. Loose deliveries can release the pressure created by a good opening spell.

CricXZ analysis — Harmer's role as the match develops: His overs may affect how Bavuma rotates the fast bowlers. The balance between attacking for wickets and controlling scoring will depend on the surface and match situation. Naming a spinner in advance does not establish exactly how the pitch will behave on later days.

Why the WTC Context Matters

The series is part of the 2025–27 World Test Championship. CSA's season announcement places the Bangladesh Tests between South Africa's Australia assignment and their later England series. Each contest has its own selection demands; the hosts cannot treat the next two Tests simply as preparation for another opponent.

The toss, final Bangladesh XI and the first look at the surface will add evidence that was unavailable when this article was prepared. Current standings or qualification scenarios should be checked separately rather than inferred from a team ranking.
