South Africa Beat Australia by 67 Runs in First ODI

Matthew Breetzke's 112 and Marco Jansen's 75 carried South Africa to 297/8 before Keshav Maharaj took 4/20 to dismiss Australia for 230 in Durban.

Published 24 September 2026By CricXZ Sports DeskMatch Report

South Africa opened the three-match ODI series with a convincing 67-run victory over Australia at Kingsmead in Durban. After being asked to bat, the hosts recovered from 168 for five to post 297 for eight, then bowled Australia out for 230 in 41.2 overs.

Matthew Breetzke anchored the innings with 112 from 106 balls, while Marco Jansen transformed the final phase with 75 from 59. Australia raced through the powerplay, but Keshav Maharaj's controlled spell of 4/20 turned the chase decisively in South Africa's favour.

South Africa vs Australia first ODI result

South Africa: 297/8 in 50 overs

Australia: 230 all out in 41.2 overs

Result: South Africa won by 67 runs

Series: South Africa lead the three-match ODI series 1-0

Breetzke rebuilds South Africa's innings

South Africa lost early momentum after Ryan Rickelton made 27 and captain Temba Bavuma contributed 30. Breetzke absorbed the pressure, rotated the strike and punished loose bowling to keep the innings together as wickets fell around him.

He reached his century and finished with 112 from 106 deliveries, striking 14 fours and two sixes. His measured innings gave South Africa a platform after the scoreboard had slipped to 168 for five.

Breetzke and Jansen add a match-changing 107

Jansen joined Breetzke with Australia pressing for a lower-order collapse. The pair instead added 107 runs for the sixth wicket, carrying South Africa from 168/5 to 275/6 and changing the shape of the contest.

Jansen supplied the late acceleration with 75 from 59 balls, including seven fours and four sixes. Their partnership allowed South Africa to reach 297/8 despite Nathan Ellis taking 3/57 and Adam Zampa returning 2/50.

Australia make a flying start to the chase

Mitchell Marsh attacked from the outset, scoring 60 from only 36 balls. Alongside Travis Head, who made 36, he helped Australia surge to 96/1 after 10 overs and put the required rate under immediate pressure.

The chase was still strongly placed at 129 for one, but Head's dismissal at that score opened the door. South Africa tightened the field, reduced the boundary flow and began taking wickets at regular intervals.

Maharaj turns the match with 4/20

Maharaj exploited the slowing surface with accuracy and subtle changes of pace. His spell removed the middle-order resistance and ensured Australia could not rebuild after the fast start.

Cooper Connolly scored 38 and Matthew Renshaw made 44, but neither could take the chase deep enough. Maharaj finished with 4/20 from 9.2 overs, including two maidens, as Australia lost their final wicket at 230.

South Africa take a 1-0 series lead

Bjorn Fortuin and Corbin Bosch supported Maharaj with two wickets each, while Jansen and Gerald Coetzee claimed one apiece. The 67-run margin completed an emphatic turnaround after Australia's aggressive powerplay.

South Africa now carry a 1-0 lead into the second ODI, with Breetzke's century, Jansen's all-round impact and Maharaj's four-wicket spell setting the early standard for the series.

Key performances

Matthew Breetzke: 112 from 106 balls, with 14 fours and two sixes

Marco Jansen: 75 from 59 balls and 1/64

Keshav Maharaj: 4/20 from 9.2 overs

Mitchell Marsh: 60 from 36 balls

Nathan Ellis: 3/57 for Australia
