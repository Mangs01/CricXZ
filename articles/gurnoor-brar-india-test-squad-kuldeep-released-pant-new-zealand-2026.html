Gurnoor Brar Earns India Test Call-Up; Kuldeep Released, Pant Joins Squad

BCCI adds Gurnoor Brar to India's New Zealand Test squad, releases Kuldeep Yadav for domestic cricket and confirms Rishabh Pant has joined the team in Wellington.

Published 14 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskSquad News • Wellington

AI-generated CricXZ editorial illustration; not an actual match or training photograph.

Gurnoor Brar has been added to India's squad for the two-Test series against New Zealand, while Kuldeep Yadav has been released to play domestic cricket. The BCCI announced the changes on 14 November 2026 and confirmed that Rishabh Pant has joined the team in Wellington.

Brar Gets His First Test Call-Up

The ICC describes Brar's selection as his maiden Test call-up. The fast bowler has been part of India's ongoing ODI tour and will now move into preparations for the longer format.

His inclusion adds another pace option alongside Jasprit Bumrah, Mohammed Siraj, Prasidh Krishna, Akash Deep and Anshul Kamboj. That gives the management more combinations to assess before choosing the attack for Wellington.

A call-up is an opportunity to compete for a place. It does not confirm that Brar will make his Test debut in the opening match, and no final playing XI has been announced in this squad update.

Why Kuldeep Has Been Released

The board's stated reason is domestic cricket: Kuldeep is being released from the Test group to play in India. The announcement does not identify an injury as the reason for his departure.

This decision concerns the Test squad. Kuldeep remains in the updated fifth ODI squad, with his return to India following that match reported separately. Describing him as unavailable for the ODI finale would therefore misrepresent the update.

Pant Joins the Team in Wellington

Pant's arrival is a separate part of the announcement. The ICC reports that he has been declared fit after his original Test selection had been subject to fitness clearance. He has now joined the squad ahead of the series opener.

Dhruv Jurel is also in the travelling group as a wicketkeeper. Pant's availability restores a selection option; this release does not announce which wicketkeeper will play.

India's Updated Test Squad

Shubman Gill (captain), Yashasvi Jaiswal, KL Rahul (vice-captain), Devdutt Padikkal, Rishabh Pant (wicketkeeper), Dhruv Jurel (wicketkeeper), Ravindra Jadeja, Anshul Kamboj, Jasprit Bumrah, Prasidh Krishna, Akash Deep, Sai Sudharsan, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Washington Sundar, Mohammed Siraj and Gurnoor Brar.

Brar Will Miss the Fifth ODI

Brar's move to the Test group means he will not be available for the final ODI. BCCI has said no replacement will be named for that fixture.

India lead the ODI series 2-1 ahead of the Bay Oval finale on 15 November. Our fifth ODI preview covers the available pace options and the series equation. The fourth ODI report records New Zealand's 244-run victory.

What Comes Next

The Test series begins on 19 November at the Basin Reserve in Wellington, before moving to Hagley Oval in Christchurch for the second Test. New Zealand Cricket has named Tom Latham as captain of the home squad.

CricXZ view: Three different questions now shape India's preparation: how Brar fits into the pace options, which spin combination suits the selected surface, and how the management uses Pant and Jurel. Those decisions depend on conditions and team balance. The confirmed news today is the squad change and Pant's availability, rather than a settled first-Test lineup.
