India vs West Indies 4th T20I Preview: Can India Seal the Series in Hyderabad?

India carry a 2-1 lead to Hyderabad after Abhishek Sharma and Arshdeep Singh drove a 14-run victory in Indore, while West Indies need a win to keep the series alive.

12 October 2026CricXZ Sports DeskMatch Preview

CricXZ editorial illustration for the India–West Indies fourth T20I in Hyderabad.

India and West Indies meet in the fourth T20I on Wednesday, 14 October 2026, at the Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium in Hyderabad. The match begins at 7:00 PM IST (1:30 PM UTC). India lead the five-match series 2-1 and can secure the contest with another victory.

India vs West Indies 4th T20I: Match Details

Date: Wednesday, 14 October 2026
Time: 7:00 PM IST (1:30 PM UTC)
Venue: Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium, Hyderabad
Series position: India lead 2-1

India Move Ahead After an Indore Run-Fest

India responded to their Ranchi defeat by posting 223 in the third T20I at Indore. Abhishek Sharma struck 69 from 30 balls, while Ishan Kishan made 37 during a 108-run second-wicket stand.

West Indies replied with 209/7. Shimron Hetmyer made 43, Kamil Pooran scored 42 and Shamar Springer finished unbeaten on 35 from 11 balls, but Arshdeep Singh claimed 3/44 and was named Player of the Match.

Series Still Carries Momentum Swings

The series has already produced three distinct contests. India completed a rapid chase in Lucknow, West Indies answered with a record pursuit in Ranchi, and India then defended 223 in Indore. That sequence makes Hyderabad a genuine pressure match despite the hosts' advantage.

Shai Hope and Sherfane Rutherford powered West Indies' second-match comeback. India, meanwhile, have received major contributions from Shreyas Iyer, Sanju Samson, Abhishek Sharma and their varied bowling group across the series.

Hyderabad Pitch and Conditions

The fourth T20I is a night match at the Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium. The exact strip, grass cover and evening dew should be assessed closer to the toss; those match-day factors will matter more than treating historical venue scores as a guarantee.

Both attacks have conceded heavily during the series, so new-ball execution and boundary prevention could be decisive. Captains will also consider whether later dew may make gripping the ball more difficult.

West Indies: Shai Hope (c & wk), Shimron Hetmyer, Kamil Pooran, Roston Chase, Sherfane Rutherford, Quentin Sampson, Shamar Springer, Romario Shepherd, Akeal Hosein, Matthew Forde, Shamar Joseph.

Selection Questions Before the Fourth T20I

India must decide whether to retain the combination that won in Indore or use the series lead to rotate personnel. Any possible opportunity for Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Kuldeep Yadav, Ravi Bishnoi or Washington Sundar remains a selection possibility rather than confirmed team news.

West Indies may similarly review their bowling balance after conceding 223. Their batting depth remains dangerous, but reducing loose deliveries and avoiding clusters of wickets will be essential in a must-win match.

Key Battles

  • Abhishek Sharma vs West Indies' new ball: His 30-ball 69 immediately put India in control at Indore.
  • Shai Hope vs Arshdeep Singh: Hope made only five in the third T20I after his match-winning century in Ranchi.
  • Sherfane Rutherford vs India's middle-overs options: Rutherford has already shown that he can reshape a chase rapidly.
  • India's finishers vs West Indies at the death: Both teams need cleaner execution after three high-scoring matches.

Players to Watch

Abhishek Sharma: His powerplay acceleration gives India immediate scoring momentum.

Arshdeep Singh: The left-arm quick enters Hyderabad after a Player-of-the-Match performance.

Shai Hope: The West Indies captain remains central to their attempt to force a deciding fifth game.

Sherfane Rutherford: His profile combines boundary-hitting power with the ability to revive a damaged innings.

Squads

West Indies: Shai Hope (c), Amir Jangoo, Roston Chase, Matthew Forde, Shimron Hetmyer, Akeal Hosein, Shamar Joseph, Gudakesh Motie, Keemo Paul, Kamil Pooran, Rovman Powell, Sherfane Rutherford, Quentin Sampson, Romario Shepherd, Shamar Springer.

Match Outlook

India have the advantage of a 2-1 lead and the confidence of defending a large total, but West Indies' Ranchi chase proved that no target is automatically safe against their batting power. Hyderabad therefore offers India a chance to seal the series and West Indies a direct route to a Bengaluru decider.
