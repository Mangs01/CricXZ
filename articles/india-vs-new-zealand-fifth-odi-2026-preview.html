India vs New Zealand 5th ODI Preview: India Seek Series Win as Henry, Jamieson Miss Finale

India lead 2-1 before the Bay Oval finale on 15 November at 7:30 AM IST. Henry, Jamieson and Brar miss out as both sides reshape their pace attacks.

India have one more chance to turn their 2-1 lead into an outright ODI series win when they face New Zealand at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, on Sunday, 15 November 2026. The finale starts at 7:30 AM IST, with both teams changing their pace options after New Zealand's emphatic fourth-match victory.

Match Details and Series Equation

  • Match: New Zealand vs India, fifth and final ODI.
  • Date: Sunday, 15 November 2026.
  • Scheduled start: 7:30 AM IST (3:00 PM New Zealand time).
  • Venue: Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui.
  • Series: India lead 2-1; the third ODI ended without a result.

An India win would make the final score 3-1. A New Zealand win would draw the series 2-2. If the finale produces no result, India would retain their 2-1 advantage. The hosts can still deny India an outright series victory, but cannot finish ahead of them.

New Zealand Without Henry and Jamieson

New Zealand Cricket's published fifth ODI squad excludes Matt Henry and Kyle Jamieson. Their absence follows the reshuffling around Test preparation; it should not be described as a newly announced injury setback.

Josh Clarkson joins the group for the finale. Jacob Duffy and Blair Tickner are among the available seam options, while Brett Randell remains a potential international debutant. A squad place does not confirm a place in the playing XI.

India Update: Brar Leaves for Test Preparation

Gurnoor Brar will also miss the final ODI after being added to India's Test squad. The ICC confirmed on 14 November that he will join the Test group in Wellington. Mohammed Siraj and Yash Thakur give India pace alternatives, with the final combination still to be announced.

Kuldeep Yadav's release concerns the Test squad. He remains listed in India's updated fifth ODI squad; the two selection decisions should not be confused.

The Batting Response India Need

The fourth ODI ended with New Zealand defending 333 and dismissing India for 89. Will Young scored 125, while Henry took 6/27. His opening over removed Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli and Shreyas Iyer without a run on the board.

For India, the immediate tactical question is how to build a stable innings against the new ball. Henry's absence changes the opposition attack, but it does not remove the need for careful shot selection and partnerships. Shreyas continues as captain for the final ODI.

CricXZ View: Two Different Tasks
