India Women vs Pakistan Women: Women's Asia Cup 2026 Match Preview

India and Pakistan meet in a high-profile Group A contest in Dubai, with both sides carrying winning form into the Women's Asia Cup 2026 clash.

Published 4 September 2026 By CricXZ Sports Desk News

India Women and Pakistan Women are set to meet on 5 September in one of the most anticipated Group A matches of the Women's Asia Cup 2026 in Dubai. India enter the contest after winning their opening two matches, while Pakistan also began their campaign with a victory.

India carry winning momentum into Pakistan clash

India opened their Women's Asia Cup 2026 campaign with a 94-run victory over Thailand. Shafali Verma struck 64 from 36 balls before India's bowlers took control, with Deepti Sharma returning three wickets without conceding a run and Nandni Sharma taking three wickets for 18.

India followed that result with another commanding performance against Hong Kong, China. Smriti Mandhana scored 124 from 64 balls as India posted 193 for five. The innings included 14 fours and seven sixes, while Deepti Sharma later claimed three wickets for nine runs as India completed their second consecutive victory.

Mandhana and Shafali give India a major top-order threat

India's opening pair will be one of the key areas to watch against Pakistan. Shafali made an immediate impact against Thailand, while Mandhana produced a record-breaking century in the following match.

Mandhana's innings against Hong Kong, China also carried historic significance. According to the ICC, she moved to 10,880 international runs, surpassing Mithali Raj's 10,868 to become the leading run-scorer in women's international cricket. It was also her 18th international century.

Pakistan arrive with confidence after winning start

Pakistan also opened their Women's Asia Cup campaign with a victory, defeating Thailand by 35 runs. Muneeba Ali scored 54 as Pakistan built a total their bowlers were able to defend.

Pakistan's bowling attack, particularly its spin options, gives the side an important weapon in Dubai conditions. Ahead of the India match, the Pakistan camp has highlighted disciplined bowling, changes of pace and adapting to the surface as important parts of its approach.

Key players to watch in India vs Pakistan

For India, Mandhana and Shafali provide attacking options at the top of the order, while Deepti Sharma has already made a significant impact with the ball. India's bowling depth was also evident in the victory over Hong Kong, China.

Pakistan will look towards Muneeba Ali for runs at the top, while captain Fatima Sana and the team's spin resources can play important roles with the ball. Nashra Sundhu and Sadia Iqbal are among Pakistan's experienced bowling options in the tournament squad.

Group A context makes the contest important

India, Pakistan, Thailand and Hong Kong, China make up Group A of the Women's Asia Cup 2026. The top two teams from each group advance to the semi-finals, making every group-stage result important in the race for the knockout phase.

India's two victories have given them a strong start to the tournament, while Pakistan entered the India fixture with confidence after beginning their campaign with a win.

India vs Pakistan: what to watch

The contest brings together an in-form Indian batting unit and a Pakistan attack that will look to use spin, variation and disciplined bowling effectively. India's ability to maintain its aggressive top-order approach against Pakistan's bowling options could be one of the defining battles of the match.

With a major rivalry, valuable Group A points and a place in the knockout-stage race all adding context, the 5 September meeting is set to be one of the standout fixtures of the Women's Asia Cup 2026 group stage.
