Asian Games 2026: Pakistan Beat Bangladesh by 6 Wickets to Reach Men's T20 Final

In a fast-paced, rain-curtailed 13-over semifinal, Pakistan aggressively chased down Bangladesh's 111/7 with 9 balls to spare, setting up a highly anticipated Gold Medal clash.

September 2026 CricXZ Sports Desk Semifinal Report

Pakistan celebrates as they secure their spot in the Gold Medal match at the Asian Games 2026.

Pakistan stormed into the Men's T20 Final of the Asian Games 2026 with a clinical 6-wicket victory over Bangladesh in the 1st semi-final. Due to weather interruptions, the match was reduced to a 13-over-a-side sprint, where Pakistan's firepower ultimately proved too much for the Bangladeshi bowlers.

Match details and current status

  • Status: Completed
  • Venue: Korogi Sports Park, Nisshin, Japan
  • Match Format: Reduced to 13 overs per side due to rain
  • Bangladesh: 111/7 in 13.0 overs
  • Pakistan: 112/4 in 11.3 overs
  • Result: Pakistan won by 6 wickets (with 9 balls remaining)
  • Competition: Asian Games 2026 - Men's T20

Bangladesh Restricted to 111/7 in 13 Overs

Forced to bat in a truncated innings, Bangladesh's batters tried to accelerate from the very first ball. While they managed to find the boundaries, Pakistan's bowlers kept taking wickets at regular intervals, preventing any massive partnerships from flourishing. The disciplined pace and spin variations from Pakistan restricted Bangladesh to 111/7, a total that seemed slightly under par on a good batting surface.

Match Snapshot

Pakistan 112/4 (11.3 overs) beat Bangladesh 111/7 (13 overs) by 6 wickets. Pakistan qualifies for the Gold Medal Final.

Pakistan's Explosive Chase Secures Final Spot

Needing 112 runs from 78 deliveries, Pakistan’s top order wasted no time. Despite losing four wickets in their aggressive pursuit, the required run rate never became a concern. The batters capitalized on the powerplay restrictions and consistently cleared the ropes, reaching the target comfortably at 112/4 in just 11.3 overs.

What's Next?

With this impressive victory, Pakistan has confirmed their spot in the Gold Medal match, where they will face the winner of the second semifinal between India and Sri Lanka. A potential India vs Pakistan final promises to be a blockbuster event. Bangladesh, meanwhile, will move to the Bronze Medal playoff.
