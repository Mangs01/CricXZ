Asian Games 2026: Pakistan Beat Bangladesh by 6 Wickets to Reach Men's T20 Final

Pakistan recovered from 53/4 to chase Bangladesh's 111/7 in a rain-shortened semifinal. Abdul Samad and Hasan Nawaz shared an unbeaten 59-run stand to seal a six-wicket win with nine balls remaining.

Match played: 1 October 2026 CricXZ Sports Desk Semifinal Report

Pakistan reached the Asian Games men's cricket final after beating Bangladesh by six wickets in Nisshin on 1 October 2026. In a match reduced to 13 overs per side, their chase required a recovery after four early wickets.

Match details and current status

  • Status: Completed
  • Venue: Korogi Sports Park, Nisshin, Japan
  • Match Format: Reduced to 13 overs per side due to rain
  • Bangladesh: 111/7 in 13.0 overs
  • Pakistan: 112/4 in 11.3 overs
  • Result: Pakistan won by 6 wickets (with 9 balls remaining)
  • Competition: Asian Games 2026 - Men's T20

Bangladesh Restricted to 111/7 in 13 Overs

Bangladesh made 111/7 from 13 overs after Pakistan chose to field. Towhid Hridoy contributed 42 from 25 balls and Saif Hassan made 19. Sufyan Moqim took 3/26, while Arafat Minhas returned 2/23.

Match Snapshot

Pakistan 112/4 (11.3 overs) beat Bangladesh 111/7 (13 overs) by 6 wickets. Pakistan qualifies for the Gold Medal Final.

Samad and Hasan Rebuild From 53/4

Pakistan slipped to 53/4 before Abdul Samad and Hasan Nawaz added an unbroken 59 for the fifth wicket. Samad finished on 30 not out from 15 balls and Hasan on 27 not out from 22. Their partnership took Pakistan to 112/4 in 11.3 overs, with nine deliveries unused.

What's Next?

With this impressive victory, Pakistan has confirmed their spot in the Gold Medal match, where they will face the winner of the second semifinal between India and Sri Lanka. A potential India vs Pakistan final promises to be a blockbuster event. Bangladesh, meanwhile, will move to the Bronze Medal playoff.
