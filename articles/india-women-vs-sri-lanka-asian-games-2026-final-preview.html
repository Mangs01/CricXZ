India Women vs Sri Lanka Women: Asian Games 2026 Gold Medal Final Preview

Defending champions India meet Sri Lanka for the women's cricket gold medal after Shafali Verma's unbeaten century dominated the semi-final against Bangladesh.

Published 22 September 2026 By CricXZ Sports Desk News

India Women will face Sri Lanka Women in the Asian Games 2026 cricket gold-medal final on 22 September after both teams won their semi-finals in Japan.

India reached the final with a 114-run victory over Bangladesh, powered by Shafali Verma's unbeaten 100 from 49 balls. Sri Lanka defeated Pakistan in the other semi-final to set up a meeting between the two leading sides.

India Women vs Sri Lanka Women final overview

Match: India Women vs Sri Lanka Women

Stage: Asian Games 2026 women's cricket gold-medal final

Date: 22 September 2026

Venue: Kōrogi Sports Park, Nisshin, Japan

Status: Scheduled; playing XIs and result pending

Shafali Verma takes India into the final

Shafali produced the defining innings of India's semi-final against Bangladesh. She reached her hundred from 49 balls and finished unbeaten as India posted 195/4 from 20 overs.

Harmanpreet Kaur added 40 and Smriti Mandhana scored 37, giving India a total that placed Bangladesh under immediate scoreboard pressure.

Bangladesh were then dismissed for 81. Nandni Sharma and Deepti Sharma took three wickets each as India completed a 114-run victory, their largest winning margin against a full-member opponent in women's T20 internationals at the time.

Sri Lanka stand between India and another gold medal

Sri Lanka advanced by defeating Pakistan in the other semi-final. Their batting threat is led by captain Chamari Athapaththu, while their bowling attack has the control and variety required for a knockout contest.

India enter as defending Asian Games champions, but the final presents a fresh contest with the gold medal decided on the day.

Key players to watch

Shafali Verma

Shafali's century against Bangladesh followed her unbeaten 40 from 15 balls in India's earlier victory over Japan. Her powerplay scoring can determine the pace of the final.

Smriti Mandhana

Mandhana gives India left-handed balance at the top of the order and has the experience to control an innings if early wickets fall.

Chamari Athapaththu

Sri Lanka's captain remains their most influential attacking batter and a major wicket for India's bowlers.

Rain could influence the gold-medal match

Rain has affected cricket in Japan during the tournament, so the weather will remain an important factor. Reports ahead of the final indicated that no reserve day was available.

If conditions allow a full match, India will aim to use the same batting depth and spin control that carried them through the semi-final. Any reduction in overs would increase the importance of the powerplay and make early wickets even more valuable.

India target back-to-back Asian Games titles

India won the women's cricket gold medal at the previous Asian Games and now have an opportunity to defend the title. Their route to the 2026 final included an eight-wicket victory over Japan followed by the record 114-run semi-final win over Bangladesh.

Shafali's recent performances also add new context to her CricXZ player profile, while India and Sri Lanka recently met during India's successful Women's Asia Cup 2026 campaign.
