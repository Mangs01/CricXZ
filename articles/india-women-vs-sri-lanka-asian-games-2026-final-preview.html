India Women Beat Sri Lanka by 147 Runs to Win Asian Games 2026 Gold

Smriti Mandhana's 79 and Richa Ghosh's unbeaten 49 powered India to 216/3 before Sri Lanka were dismissed for 69 in the gold-medal final.

Published 22 September 2026 By CricXZ Sports Desk News

India Women retained the Asian Games cricket gold medal with a dominant 147-run victory over Sri Lanka Women in the 2026 final at Kōrogi Sports Park in Nisshin, Japan.

India posted 216/3 from 20 overs after choosing to bat. Sri Lanka were then bowled out for 69 in 15.4 overs as Harmanpreet Kaur's team secured India's first gold medal of the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games.

India Women vs Sri Lanka Women final score

India Women: 216/3 in 20 overs

Sri Lanka Women: 69 all out in 15.4 overs

Result: India Women won by 147 runs

Venue: Kōrogi Sports Park, Nisshin, Japan

Date: 22 September 2026

Mandhana and Shafali launch India's record total

Smriti Mandhana and Shafali Verma gave India complete control with a 99-run opening partnership. Mandhana scored 79, striking five fours and seven sixes, while Shafali contributed 48.

Their partnership created the platform for India's 216/3, the highest total recorded in Asian Games women's cricket at the time.

Richa Ghosh finishes the innings with 49 not out

Richa Ghosh accelerated through the final overs and finished unbeaten on 49. Her late hitting ensured India converted the strong opening stand into a target of 217.

Captain Harmanpreet Kaur added 19 as India lost only three wickets across the innings.

India bowl Sri Lanka out for 69

Sri Lanka's chase never recovered after India struck regularly in the powerplay. The required scoring rate continued to rise, and the innings ended at 69 in 15.4 overs.

Deepti Sharma led the bowling figures with three wickets. Shreyanka Patil, Sree Charani and Shafali Verma took two wickets each as India completed a comprehensive all-round performance.

A 147-run victory in the gold-medal match

The 147-run margin reflected India's control of every phase of the final. The openers dominated the first half of the innings, Richa supplied the finishing power and the bowlers did not allow Sri Lanka to build a meaningful partnership.

The victory followed India's 114-run semi-final win over Bangladesh, when Shafali scored an unbeaten 100 from 49 balls.

India retain the Asian Games women's cricket title

India also won the women's cricket gold at the previous Asian Games. The 2026 triumph therefore gave the team consecutive titles and made India one of the competition's two-time champions.

India's route through the knockout tournament included an eight-wicket victory over hosts Japan, the emphatic semi-final win over Bangladesh and the 147-run victory over Sri Lanka.

Another major title after the Women's Asia Cup

The gold medal arrived shortly after India defeated Sri Lanka by 72 runs in the Women's Asia Cup 2026 final. Across the two finals, India's top-order aggression and spin-bowling depth proved decisive.

Mandhana's 79 underlined the value documented in her CricXZ player profile, while Shafali followed her semi-final century with another important contribution in the title match.
