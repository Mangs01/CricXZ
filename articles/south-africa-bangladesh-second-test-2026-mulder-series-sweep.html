SOUTH AFRICA vs BANGLADESH 2026 • 2ND TEST RESULT

Mulder’s 7/31 Seals South Africa’s 2–0 Test Sweep Against Bangladesh

A century and seven-wicket haul from Wiaan Mulder powered an innings victory in Centurion.

25 November 2026CricXZ Sports DeskTest Series Report

AI-generated CricXZ editorial illustration; not an actual match photograph. Artwork illustrates the completed Test result.

South Africa completed a 2–0 Test series sweep against Bangladesh with an innings-and-219-run victory at SuperSport Park, Centurion, on 25 November 2026. Wiaan Mulder followed his hundred with 7/31 as Bangladesh were dismissed for 184 in their second innings.

Centurion Test: Final Scores

Bangladesh: 91 and 184
South Africa: 494/7 declared
Result: South Africa won by an innings and 219 runs
Series: South Africa won 2–0
Mulder: 100 runs and second-innings figures of 7/31.

A 403-Run Lead Leaves Bangladesh Under Pressure

South Africa declared at their overnight 494/7 after Bangladesh’s first-innings 91. That left a 403-run deficit for the visitors to erase before they could make South Africa bat again.

Marques Ackerman scored 150, while Mulder made 100 from 178 balls. Tony de Zorzi contributed 66 and Marco Jansen finished unbeaten on 67 in the hosts’ only innings.

Mulder Takes Seven to Close Out the Match

Bangladesh’s second innings began with a 58-run opening partnership, but they could not turn that start into a sustained recovery. Mulder’s seven wickets drove the collapse to 184 and brought the match to an end on the third day.

The ICC described the performance as his maiden Test five-wicket haul. The seven-for followed his century with the bat, giving South Africa decisive contributions from the same player in both phases.

How the Innings Margin Works

Bangladesh’s two totals add up to 275. South Africa scored 494 in one innings, leaving a difference of 219 runs: 494 − (91 + 184) = 219.

That is why the result is an innings and 219 runs, rather than a runs-only victory. These calculations explain the final margin; they are not additional scorecard figures.

A Series Sweep and a Stronger WTC Position

South Africa had won the opening Test in Johannesburg by nine wickets. The Centurion result completed the clean sweep and strengthened their position at the top of the World Test Championship standings, according to the ICC’s 25 November report.

This report does not equate that position with confirmed qualification for the WTC final. Our earlier India qualification explainer is a dated snapshot after the Wellington draw, rather than a live standings table.

CricXZ Analysis: The Value of an All-Round Contribution

A large first-innings advantage changes the choices available to the fielding side. Bowlers can keep searching for wickets with more runs in hand, while the batting team must balance survival against the need to reduce the deficit.

Mulder’s performance mattered at both ends of that equation: his runs helped build the advantage and his wickets prevented Bangladesh extending the contest. The result can be explained through those contributions without assuming that the same pattern will repeat against a different opponent. This section is CricXZ editorial analysis.
