India Begins Preparation for Upcoming Test Series

📅 July 2026   |   ✍ CricXZ Editorial Team

The Indian cricket team has begun preparations for the upcoming Test series with a focus on training, fitness and match readiness.

India focuses on preparation

The preparation phase gives the players an opportunity to work on their batting, bowling and fielding skills ahead of the series.

Focus on batting and bowling

Training sessions can play an important role in helping players prepare for different match situations and conditions.

Young players and senior stars

A balanced combination of experienced players and emerging talent can provide the team with additional options during a long Test series.

What to expect from the series

Cricket fans will be closely following the team's preparation and the developments leading up to the first Test.
