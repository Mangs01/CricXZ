India begins preparation for upcoming Test Series

📅 July 2026 | ✍ CricXZ Editorial Team

The Indian cricket team has officially started preparations for the upcoming Test series with an intensive training camp.

Players participated in batting, bowling and fielding sessions under the supervision of the coaching staff.

Senior players focused on match simulation while young players worked on improving technique and fitness.

The coaching staff believes that the preparation camp will help India enter the series with confidence.

Fans are eagerly waiting for the first Test match which is expected to attract millions of viewers worldwide.
