🇮🇳 TEAM INDIA

India prepares for upcoming Test Series

Team India begins preparations with intensive practice sessions ahead of the upcoming Test series.

📅 July 2026 ✍️ CricXZ Sports Desk 🏏 Test Cricket

Team India is stepping up preparations for its upcoming Test assignment as players and support staff focus on building rhythm, fitness and match readiness.

Team India begins preparations

The Indian team has started its preparation with focused training sessions designed to improve batting consistency, bowling discipline and overall match fitness.

Test cricket demands patience, concentration and the ability to perform over long periods. The preparation phase therefore remains an important part of India's campaign.

Focus on batting and bowling

India's batting unit will be looking to build strong partnerships while the bowling attack will focus on maintaining pressure throughout the innings.

The combination of experienced campaigners and emerging players could provide India with a balanced squad for the upcoming challenge.

Building momentum before the series

Preparation matches and training sessions can play an important role in helping players adapt to conditions and understand their roles within the team.

With the series approaching, supporters will be watching closely for squad updates, player form and other developments surrounding Team India.
