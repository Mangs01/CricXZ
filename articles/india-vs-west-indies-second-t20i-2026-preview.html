India vs West Indies 2nd T20I Preview: Playing XI, Ranchi Pitch and Key Battles

India carry a 1-0 series lead into Ranchi after an eight-wicket win in Lucknow, while West Indies need a sharper powerplay performance to level the five-match contest.

8 October 2026CricXZ Sports DeskMatch Preview

CricXZ editorial illustration for the India–West Indies second T20I in Ranchi.

India and West Indies meet in the second T20I on Friday, 9 October 2026, at the JSCA International Stadium Complex in Ranchi. The match starts at 7:00 PM IST, with India leading the five-match series 1-0 after completing a 172-run chase in only 14.4 overs in Lucknow.

India vs West Indies 2nd T20I: Match Details

Date: Friday, 9 October 2026
Time: 7:00 PM IST (1:30 PM UTC)
Venue: JSCA International Stadium Complex, Ranchi
Series position: India lead 1-0

India Arrive With a 1-0 Lead

India's emphatic eight-wicket victory in the first T20I was built on a disciplined bowling performance followed by Shreyas Iyer's unbeaten 102 from 43 balls. The captain and Ishan Kishan added an unbroken 137-run partnership as India reached 172/2 with 32 balls remaining.

West Indies had been dismissed for 171 in 19.1 overs despite half-centuries from captain Shai Hope and Sherfane Rutherford. India therefore enter Ranchi with momentum, but the short turnaround also gives the visitors an immediate chance to adjust their plans.

Shreyas Iyer Sets the Standard

Shreyas Iyer transformed the opening match after India lost two early wickets. His maiden T20I century included 10 fours and six sixes and ensured the chase never became a contest in the second half of the innings.

West Indies must find a way to deny him comfortable scoring areas in Ranchi. Their new-ball bowlers created an opening in Lucknow, but the attack could not break the Iyer–Kishan partnership or slow the scoring rate through the middle overs.

Naman Dhir Gives India Another Bowling Option

Naman Dhir returned 3/31 on his T20I debut and played an important role in preventing West Indies from batting through their full allocation. His performance strengthened India's collection of spin and all-round options alongside Axar Patel and Kuldeep Yadav.

India could retain the winning combination. Any rotation, however, will only be confirmed at the toss; the squad also includes Ravi Bishnoi, Washington Sundar, Mayank Yadav and Prince Yadav.

West Indies Need More From the Powerplay

West Indies slipped to 44/3 in the first-match powerplay before Hope and Rutherford rebuilt the innings. Their recovery produced a competitive target, but losing early wickets reduced the freedom of the middle and lower order.

A more stable start would allow players such as Shimron Hetmyer, Rovman Powell, Rutherford and Romario Shepherd to attack later in the innings. With the ball, West Indies need sustained pressure after making an early breakthrough rather than allowing another decisive partnership to develop.

Ranchi Pitch and Conditions

The exact match strip and weather conditions should be assessed closer to the toss. At the JSCA venue, teams will need to judge the pace of the surface early and plan for a night game in which dew may influence grip and ball control. The toss could therefore affect whether the captains prefer to set a target or chase.

Because pitch behaviour can vary between matches, this preview does not treat historical venue trends as a guarantee. The final playing surface, boundary dimensions and evening conditions will be more relevant than older scorecards.

India: Abhishek Sharma, Sanju Samson, Ishan Kishan (wk), Shreyas Iyer (c), Tilak Varma, Shivam Dube, Naman Dhir, Axar Patel, Kuldeep Yadav, Arshdeep Singh, Mayank Yadav.

West Indies: Kamil Pooran, Shai Hope (c & wk), Shimron Hetmyer, Roston Chase, Rovman Powell, Sherfane Rutherford, Romario Shepherd, Matthew Forde, Akeal Hosein, Gudakesh Motie, Shamar Joseph.

These are projected combinations based on the available squads and first-match roles, not confirmed XIs. Official teams will be announced at the toss.

Key Battles

  • Shreyas Iyer vs West Indies' spin attack: The visitors need a better plan after Iyer controlled both pace and spin in Lucknow.
  • Shai Hope vs India's new ball: Hope's ability to survive the powerplay and still score quickly is central to West Indies' response.
  • Sherfane Rutherford vs India's middle-overs bowlers: Rutherford showed his power in the opener, but needs greater support around him.
  • Naman Dhir and Kuldeep Yadav vs West Indies' right-handers: India's spin options can again shape the middle overs.

Players to Watch

Shreyas Iyer: The India captain enters the match after an unbeaten century and will again anchor a powerful middle order.

Ishan Kishan: His unbeaten supporting innings in Lucknow helped India maintain an exceptional scoring rate without further risk.

Shai Hope: West Indies need their captain to combine stability with intent after his first-match fifty.

Sherfane Rutherford: His 56 demonstrated the damage he can cause if West Indies provide him with a stronger platform.

Full Squads

India: Shreyas Iyer (c), Abhishek Sharma, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Ishan Kishan (wk), Sanju Samson (wk), Tilak Varma (vc), Shivam Dube, Kuldeep Yadav, Axar Patel, Washington Sundar, Ravi Bishnoi, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Naman Dhir, Arshdeep Singh, Prince Yadav, Mayank Yadav.

West Indies: Shai Hope (c), Amir Jangoo, Roston Chase, Matthew Forde, Shimron Hetmyer, Akeal Hosein, Shamar Joseph, Gudakesh Motie, Keemo Paul, Kamil Pooran, Rovman Powell, Sherfane Rutherford, Quentin Sampson, Romario Shepherd, Shamar Springer. Jewel Andrew remains under observation following a finger injury.

Match Outlook

India start as the form side after dominating the first chase, but West Indies have enough power to level the series if they protect wickets in the powerplay and apply pressure for longer periods with the ball. The toss, final pitch and confirmed XIs will refine the matchup; India nevertheless carry the clearer momentum into Ranchi.
